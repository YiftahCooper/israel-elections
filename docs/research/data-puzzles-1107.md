## 1. Polling stations: voting places and counting rows are different units

**2021:** our **12,926** records are exactly **12,127 ordinary stations + 799 central external-envelope counting rows** (locality code 9999). Those 799 rows pool ballots cast away from the assigned station; they are not 799 hospitals, army stations, embassies or other physical voting places. The CEC's [2021 annual report, pp.7–8](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/freedom-of-info-reports/he/freedom-of-info_free-of-info2021.pdf) gives a fully reconcilable physical total: **13,756 = 12,127 ordinary + 309 hospital + 61 prison/detention + 789 COVID/isolation + 368 care-home/sheltered-housing + 102 overseas**. Its footnote explicitly excludes army bases; accessible stations are subsets, not extra stations. Thus the annual report exceeds our result-row count by **830 = 1,629 special voting stations − 799 central counting rows**. The [history summary](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/election-committee-history?chapterIndex=2) instead says **13,685**, also reported before voting; that is **71 below** the annual report and **759 above** our rows. The exact 71-category bridge was not found: calling all 759 “missing special stations” would be wrong. **2015:** **10,414 = 10,119 ordinary + 295 central-envelope rows** (code 875). The CEC's [voter-information table](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/knesset20-elections-info) defines **10,372 domestic = 10,119 ordinary + 196 hospital + 57 prison**, plus **98 overseas** (10,470 combined). The history table's 10,119 therefore counts ordinary stations. The [Comptroller's 10,464](https://www.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2016-66C-314-Veadaa.pdf), pp.1811/1849, still differs by six from that combined CEC total; no source establishes why. **Recommendation:** retain the existing numeric fields for compatibility, label them **“Polling result rows / רשומות תוצאות לפי קלפי”**, and explain the ordinary/central split. Use 13,756 only under the annual report's explicit physical scope. Do not add accessible subsets or manufacture a merged-box explanation. Dataset status notes now give the exact splits; the UI caption remains an integration recommendation.

## 2. The 2013 vote versions and 1961 electorate

**2013:** keep **885,163 / 78,974 / 18,939** for the app's CEC results series: all **10,109 polling rows**, all **1,184 independently published locality rows**, and the separately aggregated **228 external-envelope rows** reconcile to these figures and 3,792,742 valid votes. The alternative **885,054 / 79,081 / 18,941** is not merely an isolated typesetter's error: the [original Gazette, YP 6540 p.2492](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/election-committee-history/he/knesset19-results.pdf) **and the CEC's [form-30 allocation appendix, pp.3–4 and 12](https://www.gov.il/apps/elections/elections-knesset-19/heb/list/ResultsAppendix.pdf)** use it, including in the allocation arithmetic. This is strong evidence of two official data versions; it does not date or explain a correction. The differences are +109, −107, −2, net zero; seats are unchanged. [YP 6546 p.2874, 12 February 2013](https://olaw.org.il/yalkut/yalkut-6546.pdf) explicitly corrects **only the grand total**, 3,792,472 → 3,792,742. It does not change a list vote. The published statutory declaration remains the Gazette as corrected by that notice; a different webpage does not itself document an amendment to it. No inspected correction or court decision authorises the three list substitutions, so I cannot certify them as a legally amended declaration or attribute them to a specific recount. The app's **exception** means “use the internally reconciled official results-table series for analysis, disclose the different statutory totals”; it does not mean a court has endorsed those substitutions. **1961:** retain **1,271,285**, the CEC-sourced [CBS table 27.2](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/publications/doclib/2024/hnatonelectionstheknessetandgovernment.27/st27_02.pdf), consistent with [IDI](https://www.idi.org.il/policy/parties-and-elections/elections/1961/). Indexed CEC historical tables also give that electorate, so this is not simply CBS versus the election authority. Their 83.0% turnout entry conflicts with CBS’s 81.6%; they corroborate the electorate only, not the entire row. The [Knesset English summary's 1,274,280](https://m.knesset.gov.il/en/About/History/Pages/KnessetHistory.aspx?kns=5) is 2,995 higher. A summary/transcription error is plausible, but neither a typo nor an electoral-roll adjustment was documented. The specialist statistical series is the better-supported choice; no numerical change or owner fact-selection is needed.
