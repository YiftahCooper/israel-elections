# Historical archive access options and owner assistance

Research date: **7 November 2026**. Scope: Hebrew/English evidence for **1949–1992** presidential consultations, coalition membership, outside support, investiture votes and faction changes. This is an access assessment, **not a completed historical dataset or legal opinion**. Preserve [archive-access-routes.md](archive-access-routes.md), which contains earlier endpoint tests, query parameters and document hashes.

**Recommended:** start targeted JTA reading and IDI cross-checks now; ask the owner for a small packet of Knesset/ISA primary records; ask NLI to resolve newspaper access and approve any proposed export before building automation. Do not buy a subscription until its exact historical holdings and permitted project use are confirmed.

## Evidence and decision rules

- **O** = page opened and relevant text inspected through the web research tool today; **I** = official/institutional search-index text only, sometimes followed by a failed open; **P** = inherited project evidence, read locally today, not independently repeated; **U** = unverified candidate. A successfully opened catalogue calendar is not a successfully downloaded newspaper scan.
- No direct archive HTTP probes were made in this assignment. The web discovery tool cannot set the required UA or establish the origin response context; its results are discovery/documentation evidence, not successful custom-UA API tests. Reported refusals below are tool observations. Future direct approved requests must use `israel-elections-explorer/0.1 (+https://github.com/YiftahCooper/israel-elections)` and never the owner's email.
- No CAPTCHA/bot-challenge execution, login/paywall circumvention, proxies, IP rotation, credentials, account registrations, payments or messages. Stop on refusal; honour 429 and Retry-After. English Wikipedia was not opened or used as evidence; incidental search results were excluded.
- **Automated access:** an explicit supported API may authorize a method without authorizing all content or bulk reuse. Where permission was not established, the recommendation is manual reading or asking the provider, not interpreting silence as permission. Institutional access must be the owner's own eligibility, never borrowed credentials or a network-location workaround.
- Success means acquiring an attributable, readable page for each disputed fact, with its date/round and allowed retention/use. Catalogue coverage and search hits alone do not meet that test.
- Ranking below is an estimate of useful evidence per researcher effort, **not measured coverage or provider turnaround**. Sources are grouped by underlying collection; TAU/NLI JPress and copied/syndicated articles are not independent corroboration.

## What evidence actually answers each question

| Question | Best record | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Each faction's recommendation to the President | Consultation minutes/letters; explicit dated contemporary reporting | Do not infer from later coalition membership or confidence votes. Separate initial post-election rounds from mid-term replacements and failed mandates; 1949 needs its own constitutional context. |
| Coalition membership / outside support | Formation agreements, government statement, dated support agreements and changes | Cabinet representation, formal coalition membership, external support and arranged abstention are separate facts. Record intervals. |
| Investiture tally and each MK's vote | Full Divrei HaKnesset session, including voting pages | A total is not a nominal roll call. Early protocols may report totals without names; ask whether a named vote was recorded rather than reconstructing one. |
| Faction changes | Knesset faction notices/committee decisions and effective dates | The election list, parliamentary faction and constituent party need not be identical. A yearbook is a dated snapshot. |

## Ranked route assessment

Scores: yield **Y** 1–5 for the open questions; ease **E** 1–5 (5 = least effort). Priority = Y × E. They are analyst judgments conditional on access, not empirical probabilities. Reading and extracting one case usually takes longer than opening its source.
