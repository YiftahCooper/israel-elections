## What to use

**Hebrew:** use **H1** as the short attributed quotation in explanatory material; use **H2** when the additional multi-party explanation is needed. H3 is the preferable verbatim legal alternative if reuse clearance for the textbooks is undesirable. For the actual warning, use original wording with source links rather than trying to turn any excerpt into a statement its author never made.

**English:** use **E1**, with attribution to *Guide to Knesset Elections* and the publication credit above. **E2** is the shorter alternative if the surrounding interface already explains what a joint list is. For a persistent product warning, prefer an original adaptation; no permissive republication licence for these guides was verified.

Suggested **original** warning, incorporating the complete point:

> **HE:** הקול ניתן לרשימה כולה. תוצאות הבחירות אינן מגלות איזו מפלגה בתוכה העדיף כל בוחר — הפיצול כאן הוא הנחה לתרחיש.

> **EN:** A vote is for the whole list. Election results do not show which party within it each voter preferred—this split is a scenario assumption.

Optional information text: **“ארגז החול מדמה את החשבון שמרכיב את הכנסת, לא את כוונת הבוחרים.” / “The sandbox models the arithmetic of the Knesset, not voter intention.”** These are our sentences, not attributed quotations or translations of E1. A proportional-to-seats allocation is still an assumption; surveys can estimate preferences but cannot make them an observed breakdown of the official list vote.

## Reuse check

- **H1:** the textbook's copyright page, **PDF p. 2**, reserves rights to the Ministry of Education (2016) and separately identifies third-party rights. Public download is **not** an open licence. The short quotation is documented here for research; no blanket permission for permanent UI republication was established.
- **H2:** copyright page, **PDF p. 3**, reserves rights to Maggie Publishers and expressly restricts reproduction, translation and other reuse without written permission. Prefer independently written factual explanation for the product; attribution alone does not establish permission.
- **H3:** [Copyright Act, §6, WIPO Lex text](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/202010) excludes statutory, regulatory and judicial decision text from copyright. This concerns the **law's words**, not a publisher's layout, commentary or branding. The same source's §19 describes conditional fair use, including quotation; it provides no automatic word-count safe harbour. The inspected WIPO translation is historical, so this is a bounded reuse check, not comprehensive current legal clearance.
- **H4:** the PDF has no identified open-content licence. The Knesset [website terms](https://main.knesset.gov.il/About/pages/termsofuse.aspx) were found in search but inaccessible on opening; the snippet mentions commercial-use restrictions, which were **not** independently verified as complete terms. Do not treat a research-center report as statutory text under §6 simply because the Knesset published it.
- **H5:** [IDI's general terms, “זכויות יוצרים”](https://www.idi.org.il/about/terms-of-use/), directly inspected, require prior written consent for copying/quotation and require source attribution. No Creative Commons licence found for this article. Again, do not imply attribution itself clears reuse.
- **E1/E2:** no open reuse licence found in either inspected PDF; the general gov.il terms endpoint returned 403. Their official status alone does not establish that explanatory prose falls under §6. Adapt the facts independently, cite the guide, and avoid logos or implied endorsement. Verbatim product use would need an applicable quotation exception or permission, not an invented licence.
- **International references:** [IPU terms](https://www.ipu.org/terms-use), directly inspected, distinguish reserved website rights, research/personal reuse, and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for publications; commercial use and adaptation conditions matter. Do not assume all IPU material is unrestricted. ACE's indexed copyright notice could not be fetched, so its reuse conditions remain unverified.

These restrictions affect the choice between **verbatim product text** and **original factual explanation**; they are not a claim that every short research quotation requires permission. No rights holder was contacted and no permission was obtained.

## Search coverage and remaining gap

The CEC's voter information and election-overview pages, the statutory list provisions, Knesset lexicon and ממ״מ, IDI's closed-list explanations, the CEC's 2022 rulings compilation, Ministry-hosted civics books, ACE and IPU were searched. CEC overview/FAQ web pages returned 403; the Knesset lexicon returned a geographic-unavailability page; ACE's discovered pages returned 404/503 or could not be opened; archived IPU Parline timed out. None of those access controls was bypassed. The current [IPU Israel electoral-system page](https://data.ipu.org/parliament/IL/IL-LC01/elections/electoral-system/) was inspected but gives the List PR classification rather than a useful warning sentence. IDI's [2026 English candidate-selection explanation](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/65619), inspected directly, likewise explains the ballot but supplies no better complete caveat.

No inspected source provided a ready-made sentence containing **all three** propositions: whole-list vote, unrecorded constituent-party preference, and a modelled split being an assumption. This is a scoped **not found**, not a claim that no such wording exists. Surplus-vote agreements and the allocation of elected seats concern different calculations; they do not record which constituent party each joint-list voter favoured. Do not borrow their “distribution” language as if it described observable constituent-party votes.

Evidence-to-conclusion map: **E1 + H2** directly support multi-party list voting (high confidence); **H3 + E1's results section, PDF p. 5**, support the list as the voting/counting unit (high confidence); the unobserved preference split and scenario warning are **our structural inference**, not a borrowed quotation. H4/H5 are contextual, not direct proof of that inference. Direct reuse permission remains source-specific and unresolved for the explanatory prose.
