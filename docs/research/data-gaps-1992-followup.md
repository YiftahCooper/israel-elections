## Searches and stopping limits

Searched Hebrew and English combinations of Herzog, Rabin, 1992, consultations, 69, Tsomet and recommendations; targeted State Archives catalogue, President's House, Knesset and public Haaretz results. The Walla article supplies the most actionable original-record lead. Search results for other periods and unrelated documents were excluded. English Wikipedia results were not opened or used. Hebrew Wikipedia was not used in this follow-up. No NLI access block was bypassed and no rate-limit retry loop was used.

Web search/open tools do not expose configurable User-Agent headers; no direct HTTP downloader was used in this round. Any subsequent direct retrieval must send `israel-elections-explorer/0.1 (+https://github.com/YiftahCooper/israel-elections)` and stop on 429/access restrictions. Search-result counts and complete archive coverage were not measured. Inspection depths and distinct families are stated per claim above; searches yielding no qualifying record establish “not found”, not absence.
