## Sources changed and retained

were read with the web reader and/or Jina, including full government and research
PDF extractions; no English Wikipedia page was opened, cited or used. Search snippets
were discovery leads, not the sole basis of newly added evidence.

  Re-read MFA's 1948–1998 timeline and 2010 history; added IDF's historical chronology

  for Shalit's release; it is not treated as official government evidence.

## Limits deliberately preserved

This is an editorial repair, not a new event-selection or formation investigation.
The original independent chronology pairs and pending list remain intact. Direct
notices establish particulars; they are not automatically counted as chronologies.
The k24 pilot's documented interim two-source qualification remains; this review
has not silently certified it as a fully strict two-chronology record.

No War of Independence event is currently included, so no 1948 event was added to
an elected term merely to demonstrate the preferred name. No invented permanent
ending is assigned to Swords of Iron, the northern front, or a ceasefire agreement.
The Yom Kippur resolution sequence is not collapsed into a single proven cessation;
Sinai bilateral cessation is distinguished from the Anglo-French ceasefire, Litani's
withdrawal from a ceasefire, and Lebanon 1985 withdrawal from a complete evacuation.
Existing date conflicts and 2026 fighting/emergency-government gaps remain explicit.
The May 2021 operation remains in k23's caretaker period, without duplication in k24.

All event text and explanatory notes were reviewed for partisan judgments. Historical
quotation and publisher titles remain verbatim, even where they contain evaluative
words. Israeli official sources are preferred evidence, not an excuse to adopt an
individual office-holder's partisan claims. No independent peer review or exhaustive
new source audit is claimed; inherited source quotes outside changed claims were
not all freshly retrieved.
