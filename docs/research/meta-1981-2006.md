# Metadata research 1981–2006 (k10–k17): joint lists, blocs, colours

Research pass of 2026-11-04 producing `data/meta/k10.json` … `k17.json`. Format and
conventions: [`data/meta/README.md`](../../data/meta/README.md).

## Sources and access

| Source | Kind | Used for | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knesset OData `ParliamentInfo.svc` — `KNS_Faction`, `KNS_PersonToPosition`, `KNS_Person` (Knessets 10–17, 3,735 position rows) | official | Post-split faction membership corroborating affiliations; identity of MKs seated at the opening; first-day ministers of each government | Works (JSON, paged) |

| knesset.gov.il web pages (MK pages, faction pages, `elections/…/list.asp` candidate lists) | official | — | **Blocked** by a bot challenge; not circumvented, no archive copies used |
| English Wikipedia | — | **Not used** (owner rule 2026-11-04). Party colours briefly gathered from its `Module:Political party` were removed before commit; none remain in any file. | — |

Every Hebrew Wikipedia quote in the JSON was checked verbatim (after stripping wiki links)
against the fetched article text by the generator; a quote that is not found fails the build.
Ranks were derived by matching the quoted member names against the dataset roster, with every
non-exact match listed in `rankNotes`.

Lists **not** treated as alliances, with reasons: Likud 1988 onward (Herut and the Liberals
merged in August 1988); Alignment 1988 (Labor only after Mapam left); Meretz 1999 and 2006
(single merged party); Progressive List for Peace 1984/1988 (founded as a merged party, per
Hebrew Wikipedia); Hadash 1992–2006 apart from its named partners (the "Jewish and Arab public

source assigns its two elected members to separate parties — left out rather than guessed);
Kadima, Gil, Centre Party, Third Way, Yisrael BaAliyah, Shas, NRP (1981–2003) and others as
single parties. Zero-seat alliances were not researched (their rosters are unavailable).

## Blocs

* **first-government** for every election: coalition at the swearing-in of the first government
  after the election (governments 19, 21, 23, 25, 27, 28, 30, 31), from the coalition tables
  of the Hebrew Wikipedia government articles (faction, start and end dates), cross-checked
  against OData ministers appointed on the government's first day.
  * 1981: Aguda in the coalition without ministers; Tehiya and Telem joined only in 1982.
  * 1984: the Alignment list joined, but its constituent **Mapam opposed** and left the faction
    on 1984-10-22 — encoded as `{ "party": "mapam" }` in the opposition. Yahad (3) counted in
    the coalition (Weizman a minister from day one).
  * 1988: Degel HaTorah in the coalition without ministers.
  * 1992: Hadash and the ADP outside the coalition; separate **blocking-bloc-1992** scheme
    (Labor + Meretz + Hadash + ADP = 61).
  * 1999: UTJ in the coalition without ministers (left 1999-09-05).
  * 2003: date 2003-02-28 (Hebrew Wikipedia: Knesset approval); OData's first ministerial
    appointments are dated 2003-02-27, and NRP and Yisrael BaAliyah ministers only from
    2003-03-03. Hebrew Wikipedia lists both factions as coalition members on formation day.
* Not produced: a sourced right/religious vs centre-left framing for 1996 and 1999 (e.g. by
  prime-ministerial endorsement). It needs per-party endorsement sources that were not gathered
  within this pass; no scheme was invented.

## Conflicts and findings

### Dataset roster errors (reported in `datasetRosterConflicts`)

Cross-checking every elected dataset rank against OData MKs (`KNS_PersonToPosition`
PositionID 43/61) found names in the dataset that belong to no MK. In each case OData shows a
different person seated in that faction from the Knesset's opening day, and the Hebrew Wikipedia
Knesset article (which lists members in list order) places that person at that rank:

| Dataset | Rank | Dataset name | MK actually seated |
|---|---|---|---|
| k10 Likud | 31, 32, 38, 46 | דרורי שרה, וירג׳מן דרור, רבי צבי, יגורי יצחק | שרה דורון, דרור זייגרמן, צבי רנר, יצחק זיגר |
| k10 Alignment | 17, 30, 33, 40, 45 | חריש משה, בן-אהרן אברהם, ורד תמר, רון אמיר, נעים עבד | משה חריף, אברהם כ"ץ-עוז, מוחמד ותד, אמרי רון, רענן נעים |
| k11 Alignment | 16, 37 | שפירא אליהו, רן נחמן | אליהו שפייזר, נחמן רז |
| k14 Likud–Gesher–Tzomet | 15, 30 | ריבלין חיים, ליבני יהודה | חיים דיין (Tzomet), יהודה לנקרי (Gesher) — **rank identity inferred** from the matching given name; both were seated from 1996-06-17 and neither appears elsewhere in the roster |

Also outside the joint lists: k11 Shinui #3 reads "ששי וידאן"; the MK was זיידאן עטשי (OData
PersonID 2523). k14's misreadings are in both `data/raw/k14/candidates.reviewed.json` and
`elected.reviewed.json`, so the "elected cross-check" there compared two identical misreadings.
These should be re-read from the gazette scans before the app shows candidate names.

**Resolved on 4 November 2026.** Every rank above was re-read from the candidate and results scans. All were
misreadings except k11 Alignment #16: the 1984 candidate notice itself prints "שפירא אליהו" (YP 3073 p. 2826),
and the results notice prints "שפייזר אליהו". The rosters now read as printed, for example 1996 #15 דיין חיים
(ריבלין ראובן is printed at #33) and #30 לנקרי יהודה. The ranks were right, so the party assignments stand.
The resolution is in `resolvedRosterConflicts`; see the [decision 0088](../decisions/0088-roster-corrections-and-odata-audit.md)
and [the audit of all elections](elected-names-odata-audit.md).

### Source disagreements

* Rafael Eitan 1984: the Hebrew Wikipedia Tehiya article counts him in Tehiya's "4 of 5"
  seats; the Tzomet article and OData (Tzomet faction from 1987-11-30) place him in Tzomet. Assigned
  to Tzomet.
* Avraham Verdiger (UTJ 1992, entered 1994): Aguda in the UTJ article, Poalei Agudat Yisrael in
  the PAI article. Outside the transcribed ranks, so no assignment is affected.
* Post-election faction ≠ election party: Hashem Mahameed (Hadash, 1996) later sat in the Balad
  faction; Michael Kleiner (Gesher, 1996) in Herut; Moshe Peled (Tzomet, 1996) in Moledet;
  Eliezer Zandberg (Tzomet) and David Magen (Gesher) in the Centre faction. Assignments follow
  the party at the election.
* Aryeh Eldad 2006: Moledet (won Moledet's internal primaries, per his biography); his Hatikva
  party came later. No conflict once dated.
* Colours: Hebrew Wikipedia is not internally uniform (UTJ #122D70 in the 13th–15th Knesset
  articles vs #182F63 in the 16th–17th; Ra'am #15793D vs #297C32; Hadash #C81F28 vs #EA1F26).
  The earlier/most frequent value is kept for stability.

## Gaps

* No official per-member constituent affiliation exists in OData for joint factions that never
  split (Likud 1981/1984, Alignment 1981, Hadash 1981–1988, Morasha, UAL 1996–2006); these rely on
  Hebrew Wikipedia and are `medium` unless a second source agrees.
* UAL 1999 rank 3 (Hashem Mahameed): no source states which component he represented — unassigned.
* Non-elected ranks of full rosters (Alignment 1984, NU 2003, UTJ 2006) are mostly unassigned; the
  Knesset candidate-list pages that might state them are bot-blocked.
* Official party colours (CEC ballot material, party logos) were not found; all colours are
  Hebrew Wikipedia conventions. Twelve constituent parties have no colour (see the README).
* Not researched: zero-seat alliances; a 1996/1999 PM-endorsement bloc scheme; Israel Democracy
  Institute party-list PDFs (cited by Hebrew Wikipedia for some lists) as a further cross-check.

## Reproduction

The JSON files were generated by a scratch script (`.backup-scratch/meta-scripts/gen.mjs`, not
committed) from OData downloads and Hebrew Wikipedia raw wikitext saved under
`.backup-scratch/meta/`. Every recorded source carries its URL and verbatim quote, so each claim
can be rechecked without the script: OData via
`https://knesset.gov.il/Odata/ParliamentInfo.svc/KNS_PersonToPosition()?$filter=KnessetNum eq N&$format=json`,
Wikipedia via the article URL (content may have changed since 2026-11-04).
