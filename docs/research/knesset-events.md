In short, this is a source-linked research draft for stepping through changes in the 24th and 25th Knessets. Silman’s departure on 6 April 2022, Zoabi’s departure and return in May, Orbach’s departure in June, and the later party exits and returns have dated entries. **Coverage is partial. This is not ready to become an automatic government-support timeline.** The missing official votes and agreements are listed below. Research date: 8 November 2026.

The proposed screen would let a reader move between dates and see what changed. For example, Silman belongs among continuing government supporters before 6 April 2022 and is shown as having left afterwards. Her Yamina faction membership is a separate question. Chikli’s 25 April legal declaration must not be used as the date when he first opposed the government.

A vote changes the picture for that vote. It does not automatically change the next day’s coalition. The same applies to opposition members who support a particular government bill. There is no judgement about personal loyalty or hidden motives here.

## How to read the records

- **Counts:** an actual, dated change in parliamentary composition or government participation, an explicit coalition exit or return, or a confidence, budget or dissolution vote. A failed survival vote still counts in its own vote history. Faction changes belong in the faction history even when support is unchanged.
- **Does not count:** a temporary boycott, bargaining threat, or a changed faction name without a membership change. These stay out of the government-support event steps.
- **Hard case:** an actual office change whose effect on continuing parliamentary support is unclear or explicitly different. Keep the fact visible for review; leave the support total blank.

The support totals below count established continuing supporters. “Other seats” is the remainder of 120; it does not mean a united opposition or a prediction of how those members will vote. The initial Bennett coalition had 62 nominal faction seats, but Chikli opposed it. The working support total is therefore 61. Alharumi’s one abstention does not by itself establish a continuing exit. This differs deliberately from the nominal-membership total in the earlier government-role research.

A dash means that the total has not been established. Do not fill it by subtracting election-list seats: Norwegian-law replacements can change the balance between constituent parties. In particular, the January 2025 Otzma exit report itself gives two possible immediate totals.

The linked source passage supports the event; the classification is this research pass’s application of the owner’s rule. It is not presented as a historian’s judgement.

## Method and limits

Searches separated formation/accession, exits, individual statements, formal faction changes, survival votes, replacement references and excluded bargaining. The challenge pass specifically checked whether office resignation meant coalition exit, whether a later return invalidated an earlier exit, and whether a reported budget approval was only first reading. This found the Arbel reversal, the UTJ dissolution votes and the September 2024 reading distinction.

Official Knesset pages were sought first, with retained official appointment and membership records used where available. Several pages returned location restrictions or empty bodies. Their search extracts are labelled **index only**, not read primary documents. They cannot by themselves raise certainty to confirmed. No proxy, challenge bypass or login workaround was used. Managed web tools do not expose their User-Agent; future direct HTTP requests must use the project’s specified User-Agent and honour rate limits.

The event records form the claim ledger: source URLs, publisher families, publication/access dates, inspected passages, certainty and the classification reason travel with each claim. Repeated Times of Israel articles are one publisher family, as are Hebrew and English Globes. Retained OData and a narrative on the same official host do not count as independent corroboration. Search-result volume, token cost and total pages reviewed were not measured. No claim of exhaustive coverage is made.

For a handoff, retain all hard cases and gaps. Promote support totals only when the cited evidence settles the continuing relationship. A visible uncertainty is more useful than a precise but invented coalition bar.
