# Candidate biography links, 1949–2026

Research of 4 November 2026 for the planned biography links. For every candidate in the app,
the data in `data/meta/bios/kNN.json` gives, where one could be confirmed, an **official or partisan
biography** (the Knesset member page; the party's own page, live or as a Wayback Machine capture
from the campaign) and a **neutral biography** (Hebrew Wikipedia, labelled secondary). The format is
in [data/meta/bios/README.md](../../data/meta/bios/README.md). The files are generated by
`scripts/research/build-bios.py` from inputs retained in `data/raw/bios/`.

**English-language Wikipedia was not used, cited or linked** (owner rule; see the source policy in
[DATA.md](../DATA.md)). **No identity is guessed**: an entry exists only when an official identifier
or one of the rules below ties the dataset name to a person; everything else is left out and the
doubtful cases are listed here.

### The Knesset member-page id

The website does not use the OData `PersonID`. OData publishes the mapping in the entity set
`KNS_MkSiteCode` (`KnsID` = PersonID, `SiteId` = the website's member id; 1,116 rows, one per person,
no duplicates). Evidence that `SiteId` is the id in the page URL:

- The archived official page `http://knesset.gov.il/mk/heb/mk.asp?mk_individual_id_t=214`
  (Wayback capture 2004-04-29) is the page of "חבר הכנסת ליברמן אביגדור"; OData maps PersonID 427
  (ליברמן אביגדור) to SiteId 214. Further spot checks are in the table below.
- The Knesset website's own API, captured by the Wayback Machine
  (`knesset.gov.il/WebSiteApi/knessetapi/Faction/GetFactionDetails?lng=he&knessetId=25&factionId=1096`,
  2022-12-19), lists the 32 Likud members of the 25th Knesset with `MkId` and name; every `MkId` maps
  through `KNS_MkSiteCode` to the OData person of the same surname (32 of 32).
- The Wayback Machine holds captures of `main.knesset.gov.il/mk/apps/mk/mk-personal-details/{n}` for
  ids in the same range; the captures themselves are the bot-challenge page, so they show the URL
  form, not the content.
- Wikidata's formatter URL for P9770 is the same pattern (secondary corroboration only).

The 47 members of the 26th Knesset who are new have a PersonID but no SiteId yet (the Knesset opens
on 10 November 2026). Their Knesset link is missing until OData publishes their site codes; re-run the
fetch then.

## Matching method

### Elected members (official)

The elected members are the candidates the app shows as elected (`electedOf`, via
`scripts/research/elected-ids.ts`; for k26 with the engine's seats). For each list:

1. Each elected name is compared with every member of that Knesset (PositionID 43/61). Tokens are
   compared after removing titles, punctuation, final-letter forms and (for the loose form) vowel
   letters; short forms of given names (יוסי/יוסף, אלי/אליהו, …) are equivalent. Scores: identical
   tokens; identical surname and compatible given name; same loose surname; a shared surname token.
2. The list's factions are learned from the unique matches (the opening faction of the matched
   members), and the match is repeated within those factions. A unique best match is accepted.
3. Every remaining elected member, and every duplicate, needs a reviewed decision in
   `data/raw/bios/identity-decisions.reviewed.json`; without one the member is not linked and the
   build reports it. There are 81 decisions: 76 high, 1 medium and 4 left out. Most rest on the 0088
   audit (earlier surnames printed in 1949–1977, printed transliterations, replacements before the
   opening). Each states its evidence.

### Other candidates who served as MKs

- **Replacements (high).** A candidate not elected at the election who served in that Knesset in
  one of the list's factions (name score at least "same loose surname and compatible given name").
- **Other Knessets (medium).** The candidate's name is identical (all tokens) to exactly one person
  who ever served, the name is unique in that election, the person served within three Knessets of
  it, and the party lineage agrees: the person sat in a faction of a list with the same ballot
  letters, or a faction whose name shares a significant word with the list's name or parties. At a
  distance of two or three Knessets the surname must also be unique among all MKs, or the person's
  Hebrew Wikipedia article must place them in this election (see below).

### Hebrew Wikipedia

- **MKs:** the Wikidata item with P9770 equal to the member's `SiteId` (1,073 items; every item's
  Hebrew label was compared with the OData name: 19 spelling variants, no conflicts), and its hewiki
  sitelink.
- **Other candidates (medium):** a Wikidata human with a hewiki article whose Hebrew label is
  identical to the candidate's name, who is the only such item, is recorded (P102) as a member of a
  party of the list, was 21–95 years old at the election, and whose Hebrew Wikipedia article has a
  sentence that names this election (the Knesset number, or the year with "בחירות") together with a
  candidacy word (רשימה, מקום, התמודד, מועמד, הוצב, נבחר, שוריין). The sentences used are retained
  with the article revision id in `data/raw/bios/hewiki-evidence.json`.

### Party biographies

Each source in `party-sources.reviewed.json` is one family of party pages for one list in one
election. A page is tied to exactly one candidate of that list by one of:

- `slug`: the Hebrew name in the page's URL (every name token in the URL matches the candidate,
  at least surname and one given name);
- `page`: the page title (before the site name) or `h1`;
- `rankcards`: a ranked list published by the party (rank and surname must both agree);
- `listpage`: a page of the party with a biography paragraph per candidate; the candidate's given
  and family names appear adjacent.

Pages that match no candidate, or more than one, are dropped. Wayback captures were taken from the
campaign period (about five months before the election day at most).

## English names (5 November 2026)

English mode shows a candidate's **official English name from the Knesset website** (the `Name` field of
`https://knesset.gov.il/WebSiteApi/knessetapi/MKs/GetMkDetailsHeader?mkId={siteId}&languageKey=en`), retained in
`data/raw/bios/knesset-site-mk-names-en.json` (1,050 members, fetched by `scripts/research/fetch-mk-names-en.py`) and
attached by `scripts/lib/build/bios.ts` as `Candidate.nameEn`. Only a certain Knesset identity takes one: a candidate matched
as an elected member or as one who entered the Knesset in the list's faction (official OData), or a high reviewed decision —
3,197 of 15,543 candidates. A name-only match of a candidate who never sat ("not elected; identical name…") does not. The site
gives today's name, which can differ from the ballot, so the registered Hebrew name is always shown beside it. Candidates who
never sat in the Knesset have no official English name and appear in Hebrew as registered; no transliteration is invented.

## Limits

- **Wayback Machine availability.** During this research the Wayback CDX and capture servers were
  intermittently unavailable (HTTP 503, timeouts, "Temporarily Offline"). Party coverage for
  2009–2022 is therefore partial; failures are retrieval limits, not evidence that a party published
  no biographies.
- **Bot challenges.** knesset.gov.il and democrats.org.il serve bot challenges to scripts; they were
  not bypassed and no proxy was used. Knesset pages are verified through the official id mapping and
  archived pages instead; the Democrats' team page is not linked.
- **Medium entries** rest on an exact name and party lineage. They are confirmed by rule, not by an
  identifier tying the candidacy to the person; the app should show them, if at all, with the
  confidence.
- Non-elected candidates of 1949–1999 are mostly missing because the released rosters of those
  elections stop at or near the last elected rank.

## Reproduction

```sh
node scripts/research/elected-ids.ts > /dev/null          # sanity check (the builder calls it)
python3 -I scripts/research/build-bios.py --report        # rebuild data/meta/bios from data/raw/bios
python3 -I scripts/research/build-bios.py --fetch-hewiki  # fetch missing Hebrew Wikipedia evidence, then rebuild
python3 -I scripts/research/build-bios.py --check         # offline: exit 1 if a rebuild would change any output
```

The rebuild is offline and reproduces the committed files exactly; `tests/research-builders.test.ts`
runs `--check` for this builder and for `build-party-links.py`. The builder also rewrites the summary
table above. Hand corrections are never made in `data/meta/bios/`; they go into reviewed inputs the
builder applies, each with its reason and source:

- `data/raw/bios/identity-decisions.reviewed.json`: the Knesset member for each elected candidate the
  name rules cannot settle (an optional `basis` keeps an audit's wording), and reviewed links for
  candidates who were not elected (since 9 November 2026: `18-עם#13`, whose party-word match stopped
  firing after the k18 audit corrected the constituent's name).
- `data/raw/bios/entry-corrections.reviewed.json`: the Knesset-link marker that the k2, k3 and k24 audits
  wrote after checking every PersonID/SiteId pair against the official mapping, and the two reviewed
  English display names of the k12 audit.
- `data/raw/bios/party-sources.reviewed.json`: party pages, as before.

The OData snapshots were fetched with curl from `https://knesset.gov.il/Odata/ParliamentInfo.svc/`
(`KNS_Person`, `KNS_PersonToPosition?$filter=PositionID eq 43 or PositionID eq 61`,
`KNS_PersonToPosition?$filter=PositionID eq 54`, `KNS_MkSiteCode`), appending `$format=json` to every
request including the `odata.nextLink` pages. Wikidata snapshots came from query.wikidata.org (the
queries are recorded in `data/raw/SOURCES.json`). Party pages were found through the Wayback CDX API
(`http://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=<domain>&matchType=domain&from=…&to=…&filter=statuscode:200`);
the research tools for that step are in `scripts/research/bios-party/`.
