## Reproduce the numerical screening

Run `node scripts/check-map-line-candidates.mjs` from the repository root (Node 24.18.0, locked proj4 2.22.0). Inputs are retained in `data/raw/map-lines/`; no future download is needed. OCHA response includes all five features (Gaza, Judea and Samaria, and no-man's-land edges). The OSM snapshots retain way identifiers, tags and unsimplified coordinates; unrelated nodes/metadata and Gaza maritime/coastal/Egypt ways are omitted. Source endpoints:

- `https://www.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/relation/7304999/full.json`
- `https://www.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/relation/1473938/full.json`
- `https://gis.unocha.org/server/rest/services/Hosted/1949_Armistice_Green_Line/FeatureServer/0/query?where=1%3D1&outFields=*&f=geojson&outSR=4326`

OSM data © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). OCHA service copyright: `OCHA_oPt`; retained for research comparison, not redistributed as a production tile layer. No publication licence for a new production derivative was established.

Method: project WGS84 vertices to UTM zone 36N, densify each candidate segment to intervals ≤25 m, measure each sample's minimum distance to the other candidate's entire segment set, then take the maximum in both directions. The discrete sampled result underestimates continuous projected Hausdorff distance by at most 12.5 m; projection distortion and original data accuracy are additional limitations. These are **screening deviations, not surveyed error bounds**. No snapping, invented connectors or averaging hides disagreements. Judea and Samaria selection is the retained OSM ways wholly within 34.7°–36° E / 31.2°–32.7° N versus OCHA West Bank and No Man's Land features. Gaza uses named land ways rather than the whole territorial polygon.
