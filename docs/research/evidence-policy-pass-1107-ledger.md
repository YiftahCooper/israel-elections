# Historical evidence retrieval — 7 November 2026

Scope: the five requested questions and bibliography verification. Research helper only; no repository metadata or source files changed. Existing five research notes, the five aftermath records, the retained 1992 extraction, and relevant 1981 audit notes were read before synthesis. “Unknown” below means not established by this retrieval, never a recommendation of nobody. A coalition vote, recommendation, coalition membership and continuing external support are different claims.

## Actionable conclusions

- **Item 2, Degel HaTorah 1988: nobody; preferred a broad Labor–Likud government.** A newly located bylined contemporary l’Unità report explicitly says neither Shamir nor Peres. Together with the already known UPI description of a unity preference, this is strong direct evidence for `none`, with the unity qualification preserved. No presidential minute recovered.
- **Item 4, Democratic List of Nazareth 1949: scholarship supports coalition affiliation without a ministry.** Galnoor–Blander’s explicit coalition diagram includes the Arab List, independently of IDI’s named five-party account. This is materially stronger than an inference from its confidence vote. Recommend `member-without-ministries` under the new scholarship policy, with the distinction between coalition affiliation and a recovered accession instrument disclosed.
- **Item 4, Yemenite Association: disputed.** The same handbook includes it in a 74-seat coalition; IDI’s five-party/73-seat account omits it. WIZO is outside the handbook’s coalition arrow. Neither source establishes a continuing WIZO support arrangement.
- **Item 5: new explicit contemporary evidence favors 15 July 1981 as tasking date, but does not eliminate the 16 July archival-letter conflict.** Do not invent a 15 July announcement / 16 July formal meeting reconciliation: the announcement may instead be 14 July, because bulletin publication and dispatch dates differ.
- **Items 1 and 2, Arab-list recommendations:** the known retrospective is explicit for each named list, including Hadash and ADP in 1992, but no independent contemporary or scholarly corroboration was recovered. Do not promote these merely by subtracting known seats from a total.
