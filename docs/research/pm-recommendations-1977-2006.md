# Recommendations to the President after the elections of 1977–2006 (k9–k17)

Research pass of 5 November 2026 for the "after the election" section. It records what each Knesset faction told
the President in the consultations before the mandate to form a government was given. It does not record the
eventual coalition. Data: a `pm-recommendations` bloc scheme in `data/meta/k10.json` (partial), `k11.json`,
`k12.json`, `k16.json` and `k17.json`, in the same format as k18–k25.

Every scheme's seats were checked against the official seat table (sum 120, every seat-winning list in exactly one
bloc). All sources are labelled `secondary` (contemporary news reports in online archives, and Hebrew Wikipedia)
except the State Archives chapter (archival) and the Knesset documents (official). No official per-faction record of
any consultation round was retrievable (see Access).

## Legal frame

* **1977–1992.** Basic Law: The Government (1968), s. 6: after consulting representatives of the Knesset factions,
  the President entrusts a Knesset member with forming a government (21 days, extendable by 21). The 1968 law
  required consultation, not a count of recommendations; the counts below are what the President or the press reported.
* **1996 and 1999: no recommendation round for the Prime Minister.** Basic Law: The Government (1992),
  s. 3(b) ([Knesset PDF](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/12/law/12_lsr_210707.pdf), official; retrieved 2026-11-05, HTTP 200,
  SHA-256 `170bee91…f987`): «ראש הממשלה מכהן מכוח היבחרו בבחירות כלליות, ארציות, ישירות, שוות וחשאיות, לפי חוק
  הבחירות לכנסת ולראש הממשלה» — "The Prime Minister serves by virtue of being elected in general, national, direct,
  equal and secret elections, under the Knesset and Prime Minister Elections Law." S. 4: whenever Knesset elections
  are held, elections for the Prime Minister are held at the same time. (The PDF's text layer prints Hebrew in visual
  order; the quote was re-read in logical order.) The Prime Minister elected on 29 May 1996 (Netanyahu) and
  17 May 1999 (Barak) presented a government without a presidential mandate. Hebrew Wikipedia's table of mandates
  (secondary) states for both: «לא נערכו התייעצויות לצורך הטלת התפקיד» — "no consultations were held for entrusting
  the task". See also [era-specific-rules.md](era-specific-rules.md) §B. No scheme was created for k14/k15.
* **2001.** The special election of 6 February 2001 elected Sharon as Prime Minister during the Fifteenth Knesset;
  there was no Knesset election and no recommendation round. Not a k-election in this app.
* **2003 onward.** Basic Law: The Government (2001), s. 7(a): the President entrusts the task after consulting the
  faction representatives (28 days, extendable by 14). The 2006 Labor delegation noted that the President is not bound
  to choose the largest party's leader (TheMarker, 2 April 2006).

## Per election

### 1977 (k9) — gap

* **Mandate:** Tuesday 7 June 1977, President Ephraim Katzir to Menachem Begin. Official: Begin's presentation of the
  government, Knesset plenum 20 June 1977 ([fs.knesset.gov.il 9_ptm_254065.pdf](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/9/Plenum/9_ptm_254065.pdf),
  HTTP 200; quote from the PDF's text layer as indexed): «ביום ג' כ"א סיון תשל"ז, 7 ביוני 1977, הואיל נשיא המדינה, מכוח
  סמכותו ועל־פי המלצתן של סיעות בכנסת המייצגות רוב מכריע של חבריה, להטיל עלי את התפקיד להרכיב את הממשלה» — "On
  7 June 1977 the President, by his authority and on the recommendation of Knesset factions representing a decisive
  majority of its members, entrusted me with forming the government."
* **Context (secondary):** [JTA, 8 June 1977](https://www.jta.org/archive/katzir-officially-asks-begin-to-form-new-government-likud-leader-urges-labor-join-him):
  Likud and Sharon's Shlomtzion, "which merged with it last week", would hold 45 seats; NRP 12 "considered virtually
  certain to join"; Agudat Yisrael's 4 subject to its Council of Sages; the DMC (15) still negotiating.
* **Gap:** which factions recommended Begin, and what the Alignment, DMC, Hadash, Shelli, Flatto-Sharon, the
  Independent Liberals, Ratz, the United Arab List and Poalei Agudat Yisrael said, was not found in any accessible
  source. Begin's "decisive majority" is not a per-faction record, and coalition membership is not used as a proxy.
  The obvious source is Davar/Maariv of 1–8 June 1977 in JPress (blocked, see Access).

### 1981 (k10) — partial scheme

* **Consultations:** 13–14 July 1981. Israel State Archives, [*Navon and the 10th Knesset election*](https://catalog.archives.gov.il/site/chapter/navon-and-10-knesset-election/)
  (archival; direct request 403, quote from a search-index extract): «ב-10 ביולי זימן נבון את סיעות הבית להתייעצויות
  ב-13 וב-14 ביולי» — "On 10 July Navon summoned the Knesset factions to consultations on 13 and 14 July." The chapter
  cites file נ-571/13 pp. 5–7 (Navon's letter to Begin of 16 July entrusting the task) and the President's diary
  נ-224/4; these scans were not reached.
* **Mandate:** 15 July 1981 (JTA, UPI; the Archives give the letter as 16 July — recorded as a date discrepancy).
* **Count:** [UPI, 14 July 1981](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1981/07/14/Begin-asked-to-form-new-Israeli-government/8570363931200/)
  quotes Navon: "61 Knesset members spoke out in favor of his forming the government". [UPI (second story)](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1981/07/14/Navon-to-ask-Begin-to-form-government/1036363931200/):
  "Begin has support in principle from three religious parties — the National Religious Party, the Tami Party and the
  Agudat Israel Party — which hold a total of 13 seats". 48 + 13 = 61. (Both UPI pages: direct request 403; quotes from
  a search-index extract.)
* **Per faction ([JTA, 15 July 1981](https://www.jta.org/archive/navon-says-he-will-invite-begin-to-form-a-new-government), verified on the page):**
  Tami "indicated to Navon that it preferred a government headed by Begin, while Moshe Dayan's two-seat Telem party
  leaned toward Labor"; Hadash "urged Navon to choose a Prime Minister who would work for the establishment of a
  Palestinian state"; Tehiya pressed for renegotiating the treaty with Egypt; Shinui and the Civil Rights Movement
  "preferred Labor to Likud but were resigned to the fact that Navon would select the party with the largest
  representation".
* **Scheme:** Begin 61 (מחל, ב, ג, ני); "Not stated in source" 59 (אמת, ו, ת, כן, הן, רצ). The Alignment's own
  recommendation (presumably Peres) was not found stated; JTA's leanings are not recommendations, so they are not
  coded as "Peres". Conflict: Hebrew Wikipedia (*הבחירות לכנסת העשירית*) says the Likud-led bloc reached 64 with
  Tehiya; the President's count was 61, so Tehiya is not placed with Begin.

### 1984 (k11) — complete

* **Consultations:** ended Friday 3 August 1984; Kach (Kahane) was not received. **Mandate:** Sunday 5 August 1984 to
  Shimon Peres (21 days + 21). The rotation agreement and the unity government (13 September 1984) came later.
* **Totals:** [JTA, 7 August 1984](https://www.jta.org/archive/peres-shamir-talks-on-forming-a-government-are-inconclusive)
  (verified; daily-bulletin PDF also kept, SHA-256 `fe6ccce5…102d`): "60 Knesset members asked Herzog last Friday to
  choose Peres to form the next government while Likud could only muster 54 MKs to ask the President to give Shamir
  the first chance."
* **Per party:** Hebrew Wikipedia, *הבחירות לכנסת האחת עשרה* (secondary): «על פרס המליצו 60 חברי כנסת להרכבת
  הממשלה (המערך, יחד, רצ, שינוי, חד"ש, הרשימה המתקדמת ואומץ) בעוד שעל שמיר המליצו 54 (הליכוד, התחייה, ש"ס, אגודת
  ישראל ומורשה), כשהמפד"ל ואבוחצירא תומכים בממשלת אחדות וכהנא לא הוזמן לבית הנשיא» — "60 MKs recommended Peres
  (Alignment, Yahad, Ratz, Shinui, Hadash, Progressive List, Ometz), 54 Shamir (Likud, Tehiya, Shas, Agudat Yisrael,
  Morasha); the NRP and Abuhatzeira supported a unity government and Kahane was not invited." Contemporary
  corroboration: [El País, 6 August 1984](https://elpais.com/diario/1984/08/06/internacional/460591203_850215.html)
  (verified) lists the same parties on each side.
* **Scheme:** Peres 60 (אמת 44, ו 4, רצ 3, ט 3, הן 3, פ 2, יש 1); Shamir 54 (מחל 41, ת 5, שס 4, ג 2, עי 2);
  no recommendation / broad government 5 (ב 4, ני 1); not invited 1 (כך).
* **Conflicts:** El País counts 59 for Labor ("los tres que aunan Ezer Weizman y el Partido Ometz" — Yahad 3 + Ometz 1
  is 4); JTA's 60 and the seat table agree with 60. Hadash's recommendation was conditional: +972 (2022, secondary)
  reports that after Herzog asked for clarification Tawfik Toubi said Hadash was "advising or recommending … that you
  charge one of the MKs from Alignment" — coded as Peres, as the President's count did.
  Mapam (6 of the 44) was still in the Alignment at the consultations; it left on 22 October 1984.

### 1988 (k12) — complete

* **Consultations:** from 7 November 1988 (US embassy cable, Israel Radio report); Shas, Agudat Yisrael and Degel
  HaTorah were given postponements to Sunday 13 November. **Mandate:** 14 November 1988 to Yitzhak Shamir.
* **Totals:** [LA Times (UPI), 14 November 1988](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1988-11-14-mn-244-story.html)
  (verified): "The decision gave Shamir the support of 63 members … Peres … could muster only 55 Knesset members".
* **Per party:** [JTA, 9 November 1988](https://www.jta.org/archive/fate-of-next-government-hangs-on-outcome-of-shas-party-session)
  (verified): Mapam, the Civil Rights Movement and Centre–Shinui "urged Herzog to approach Foreign Minister Shimon
  Peres"; Tehiya, Tsomet and Moledet "opted for a Likud government headed by Premier Yitzhak Shamir".
  [UPI, 14 November 1988](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1988/11/14/President-Chaim-Herzog-asked-Prime-Minister-Yitzhak-Shamir-to/7730595486800/)
  (403; search-index extract): Shas and Agudat Yisrael endorsed Shamir on Sunday night; the NRP "pledged its five seats
  to Likud last week"; "Degel Hatorah, pledged its two Knesset seats to a broad-based coalition of Labor and Likud".
  [LA Times, 14 November 1988](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1988-11-14-mn-53-story.html) (verified):
  40 + 11 + one religious party + three far-right parties = 63.
* **Scheme:** Shamir 63 (מחל 40, שס 6, ג 5, ב 5, ת 3, ץ 2, ט 2); Peres 55 (אמת 39, רצ 5, ו 4, מפם 3, הן 2, פ 1, ע 1);
  broad government / no recommendation 2 (עץ).
* **Weaker point:** Hadash, the Progressive List and the Arab Democratic Party (6 seats) recommended Peres per
  [+972 Magazine](https://www.972mag.com/palestinian-citizens-israel-coalition-elections/) (2022, secondary; 403 to a
  direct request): "Hadash, the Progressive List, and the Arab Democratic Party all recommended Peres for the
  premiership". It also follows arithmetically: 55 − 39 − 10 = 6. No contemporary report naming them was retrieved.
  Agudat Yisrael's later reversal (December 1988) concerns coalition talks, not the recommendation.

### 1992 (k13) — gap

* **Consultations:** began Sunday 28 June 1992 with all ten factions ([JTA, 29 June 1992](https://www.jta.org/archive/rabin-starts-building-coalition-with-haredi-parties-readay-to-talk)):
  "The consultations … are this year a formality: … Labor's Yitzhak Rabin and the parties to his left have a decisive
  bloc of 61 seats." **Mandate:** Thursday 2 July 1992 to Rabin ([LA Times](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1992-07-03-mn-1517-story.html);
  [JTA bulletin](http://pdfs.jta.org/1992/1992-07-03_127.pdf)).
* **Gap:** no source retrieved gives the number of recommendations or each faction's position (Likud, Tzomet, NRP,
  Shas, UTJ, Moledet). +972 (secondary) says Hadash and the Arab Democratic Party recommended Rabin; Meretz's
  recommendation is likely but not stated in what was read. A 61-seat "blocking bloc" is not a recommendation count, so
  no scheme was made (the existing `blocking-bloc-1992` scheme covers that framing).

### 2003 (k16) — complete

* **Consultations:** announced to start Monday 3 February 2003 (Ynet); continued to 6 February, Ra'am last that
  evening. **Mandate:** Sunday 9 February 2003 to Ariel Sharon (28 days + 14).
* **President:** Haaretz (Yehonatan Liss; page dated 8 February, text "today (Sunday)"; 403, search-index extract):
  «"87 חברי כנסת המליצו לי להטיל את המשימה על אריאל שרון"» — "87 MKs recommended that I entrust the task to Ariel Sharon."
* **Per party:** [Walla!, 6 February 2003](https://www.walla.co.il/news/israel/346012) (Haaretz report, verified):
  «עד כה המליצו סיעות הליכוד, שינוי, ש"ס, האיחוד הלאומי, מפדל, יהדות התורה, עם אחד וישראל בעלייה על שרון. נציגי
  שאר הסיעות, העבודה, מרצ, בל"ד, חד"ש-תע"ל לא התנגדו להטלת התפקיד על שרון, ולא הציעו כל מועמד אחר» — "So far Likud,
  Shinui, Shas, the National Union, NRP, UTJ, One Nation and Yisrael BaAliyah recommended Sharon. Labor, Meretz, Balad
  and Hadash–Ta'al did not object to Sharon and proposed no other candidate." [INN, 9 February 2003](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/38648)
  (verified): "Only Labor (19 seats), Meretz (6), and the Arab parties (8) did not recommend Sharon."
* **Scheme:** Sharon 87 (מחל 38, יש 15, שס 11, ל 7, ב 6, ג 5, ם 3, כן 2); no recommendation 33 (אמת 19, מרצ 6, ו 3,
  ד 3, עם 2). Mitzna (Labor) met the President on 6 February to set out Labor's conditions; no source says Labor
  recommended him. Yisrael BaAliyah merged into the Likud faction on 6 February (Elaph); it is coded by its ballot list.

### 2006 (k17) — complete

* **Consultations:** Sunday 2 April – Thursday 6 April 2006. **Mandate:** 6 April 2006 to Ehud Olmert (also stated in
  the Kadima–Labor coalition agreement, [Knesset PDF](https://main.knesset.gov.il/mk/government/documents/Coal2006avoda.pdf), official).
* **President:** [Globes, 6 April 2006](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1000081213) (verified): «קצב
  ציין, כי 78 מחברי הכנסת הבאה תמכו במועמדותו של אולמרט בסבב ההתייעצויות … וזאת ללא התנגדות, או המלצה על מועמד אחר.
  קצב הבהיר, כי יו"ר העבודה, עמיר פרץ הודיע לו טלפונית שלשום בערב, כי גם סיעת העבודה מעבירה את תמיכתה לאולמרט» —
  "78 MKs supported Olmert, with no objection or recommendation of another candidate; Peretz told him by phone two days
  earlier that Labor too was transferring its support to Olmert."
* **Per party (all verified on the page unless noted):**
  Kadima → Olmert (2 April); Labor → Peretz on 2 April (TheMarker; 403, search-index extract: «נציגי מפלגת העבודה
  המליצו … להטיל על יו"ר המפלגה, עמיר פרץ»), then Olmert from 4 April (Globes);
  Pensioners and Meretz → Olmert, 3 April ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-3235659,00.html));
  Shas → Olmert, 4 April ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-3236272,00.html));
  Likud → no one ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-3235891,00.html): "decided not to recommend anyone");
  UAL–Ta'al → no one ("They made no recommendations for the premiership");
  Yisrael Beiteinu, Likud and "all three Arab parties declined to endorse anybody" ([INN, 5 April 2006](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/101500));
  UTJ: Rabbi Elyashiv instructed its MKs to recommend Olmert ([Arutz 7, 3 April](https://www.inn.co.il/news/144780)).
* **Scheme:** Olmert 78 (כן 29, אמת 19, שס 12, זך 7, ג 6, מרצ 5); no recommendation 42 (מחל 12, ל 11, טב 9, עם 4, ו 3, ד 3).
* **Conflicts / weaker points:** National Union–NRP: Ynet says it "did not make a recommendation" and asked for two
  more days; INN says it "initially endorsed Peretz, until it became clear that he did not really intend to pursue the
  position". The President's final statement records no other candidate, so it is coded "no recommendation" with the
  conflict in the scheme notes. UTJ's meeting with the President (expected 6 April) was not found reported; its 6 seats
  follow from 78 − 72 and match the Hebrew Wikipedia breakdown («קדימה 29, העבודה 19, ש"ס 12, גיל 7, יהדות התורה 6 ומרצ 5»).

## Timeline notes (not post-election rounds)

* **1983:** Herzog entrusted Shamir after Begin's resignation (mid-term).
* **1986:** rotation — Herzog entrusted Shamir on 17 October 1986 under the 1984 agreement (mid-term).
* **1990 ("the dirty trick"):** after the Shamir government fell to a no-confidence vote on 15 March 1990, Herzog
  entrusted Peres on 20 March 1990 with 60 MKs recommending each side ([NYT, 21 March 1990](https://www.nytimes.com/1990/03/21/world/peres-chosen-to-try-to-form-israeli-cabinet.html),
  secondary): "60 Knesset members said they supported Mr. Peres …, while 60 urged Mr. Herzog to nominate … Shamir".
  Peres returned the mandate; Shamir was entrusted on 27 April 1990 (Hebrew Wikipedia table). Mid-term: no scheme.
* **1995:** Weizman entrusted Peres after Rabin's assassination (mid-term).

## Gaps and next steps

1. **1977 and 1992 per-faction positions**, and the **1981 Alignment/Telem/Shinui/Ratz/Hadash/Tehiya positions**: the
   best sources are Davar, Maariv and Haaretz in JPress (1977: 1–8 June; 1981: 14–15 July; 1992: 29 June–2 July) and
   the Israel State Archives presidential files (נ-571/13, נ-224/4 for 1981). Both need human browser access.

3. **President's Residence press releases** for 2003 and 2006 were not found online; the President's words come from
   news reports.
