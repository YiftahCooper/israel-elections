## Source families and proof limits

- **Knesset histories** are official narratives, read from the credited JVL copies. Their official
  authorship, original locator and mirror are disclosed in each registry. JVL contributes no
  independent corroboration. Its independently authored essays were not used.
- **MFA history** is the official *Facts About Israel: History* PDF (2010), read directly and
  extracted with the project's pdfjs-dist. **MFA timeline** is the official *Half a Century of
  Independence, 1948–1998*. Ordinary HTTP returned 403; the full official page was read using
  the brief's permitted Jina tool. The PDF, timeline and any other MFA representations are
  **one** family. Knesset and MFA are separately authored institutional histories, not a mirror
  pair. Their agreement supplies national-event selection, not endorsement of their framing.
- **CRS 2009** is Carol Migdalovitz's RL33476 retrospective, read on the EveryCRSReport copy.
  Its dated political history independently corroborates the 2006 war, Gaza takeover, Winograd
  report and Sharon incapacity. It is registered as official US congressional research,
  not as contemporary news. The mirror is not an additional source.
- **IDI** election retrospectives, constitutional history and democracy-history timeline are
  academic/research-institute sources. Several IDI texts remain one institutional family;
  an IDI page is never paired only with another IDI page to satisfy the rule.
- **MOD archive Time Axis 1972–1980** is an official chronology independently selecting and
  dating Lod, Munich, Entebbe and the Coastal Road attack. MOD's Agranat-report catalogue is a
  direct date record, not another national-event-selection chronology.
- **Direct date sources**: MOD Agranat-report account; Bank of Israel Annual Report 1985;
  German Federal Foreign Office's 1965 relations retrospective; Asser Institute's Eichmann
  procedural history; Israel's June 1994 CERD submission reproduced by the UN; UN General
  Assembly membership record and Resolution 3379; UNTSO's contemporary Scorpion Pass report
  S/3252. Their identity and retrieval copy are recorded in the JSON. They are not added to
  `term.chronologies` merely because they confirm a day. The UNTSO PDF was read in full with
  Jina after ordinary web fetch returned 403. Refworld's resolution text was opened with the
  web tool; an ordinary request returned 403. No challenge, authentication or paywall bypass
  was used. Search-result snippets alone did not qualify an accepted claim.

Confidence is high for the bounded facts at their recorded precision. It does **not** imply
an exact day where only a month or year was established. Year-level combined/process rows
include rights-law enactments (1992), disengagement agreements (1974), military-administration
termination (1966), the new economic policy (1962), and final Sinai withdrawal (1982).
The missing exact act/day is disclosed where relevant; no casualty count, trend statistic,
motive or causal interpretation is inferred. Further primary date work could refine these
rows without changing the chronology-based inclusion decision.

## Pilot k9 and resolved contradictions

The four existing k9 event statements and dates are preserved, with added independent MFA
selection evidence. The approval acts still rely on the Knesset history: MFA selects the
accords/treaty, but does **not** corroborate their parliamentary vote or tally. Notes explicitly
keep those claims separate. Litani, the Coastal Road attack and Peace Now now meet the pair
rule. Economic liberalisation is indeed discussed in the full Knesset history, correcting the
old pending reason; its exact policy act/date remains unverified. Settlement growth stays a
trend candidate. The Jerusalem law still lacks a second retrieved qualifying chronology.

K9's window previously followed the first cabinet (20 June 1977–5 August 1981). It now follows
the project's election-to-election convention (17 May 1977–30 June 1981), supported by the
existing election result and IPU election records. Government tenure is untouched.

Other material conflicts and limits:

- Knesset's Ma'ale Akrabim date is 18 March 1954. The contemporary UNTSO report records the
  attack and first observer visit on **17 March**, which controls the new row. No nationality
  or casualty count is inferred from competing accounts.
- IPU's incapacitating-stroke date is December 2005. CRS gives **4 January 2006** and acting-PM
  succession; the latter controls. The earlier December stroke is not substituted for incapacity.
- Knesset's Ze'evi assassination date is erroneous (23 October 2000); MFA places it in 2001.
  The candidate remains pending until a direct correct day is recorded.
- Oslo II stays at September 1995 precision; the Knesset's Taba date and the Washington signing
  ceremony are not silently treated as the same day.
- Agranat's first report date is **1 April 1974**, checked against the MOD archival account;
  the Knesset mirror's corrupted date and incorrect pre-war appointment claim are not adopted.
- MFA misnumbers the 1975 Zionism resolution as 3375. The actual UN text establishes **3379,
  10 November 1975**; the row attributes the determination to the General Assembly.
- Knesset and IDI use different nationality descriptions for October 2000 casualties. The row
  includes no casualty total. The Wadi Salib date and Kastner libel-case characterization
  remain unresolved and unpromoted.
- The 1985 Lebanon withdrawal row expressly retains the southern security zone, following
  MFA/IDI; Knesset's unqualified withdrawal wording is not copied literally into the statement.
