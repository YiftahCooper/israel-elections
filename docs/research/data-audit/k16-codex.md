Accessed: **2026-11-06**. Election: **28 January 2003**, sixteenth Knesset.
Baseline: `cf9935e` (fast-forwarded from `origin/main`, includes required `53cca36`). Auditor only: no data edits, dataset build, push, or merge into main. No other auditor's report was consulted.

## Summary and counting

**OK: 46 · WRONG: 8 · UNSUPPORTED: 2 · UNVERIFIABLE: 4 · DISPUTED: 2.**

The 46 OK units are L01–L27 plus O01–O19. Findings F01–F16 account for all non-OK counts.

Counts describe numbered audit units below, not individual JSON scalar values. Each list is one unit for results/name/roster scope; exceptions go in separate F rows and do not negate the other confirmed fields. A grouped limitation does **not** mean its individual claims were independently verified. This is a substantial but **partial independent verification**: all national votes, seats, ballot letters, retained elected ranks, and locality arithmetic were checked; external biography destinations, all affiliation details, demographic classifications, and every historical family interpretation were not independently established. Those limits are explicitly classified below.

Highest-priority corrections: first-government date; omitted Olmert succession; misleading dissolution date; רע״ש candidate #3; known joint-list name-matching counts. National vote differences for Mafdal and Lider are already disclosed source conflicts, not newly discovered arithmetic errors.
