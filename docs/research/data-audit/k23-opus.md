`data/meta/aftermath/k23.json`, `data/meta/candidate-notes.json`, `data/meta/election-context.json`,

The core numbers are sound: national totals, all 30 lists' votes/letters/names/seats, the three surplus agreements,
every roster rank, the 120 opening members, the recommendation round, the formation timeline and government 35 all
confirm against rank-1/2-quality sources. The errors are in roster transcription (hyphenated names split across PDF
lines), one wrong historical note (Norwegian law), a wrong source attribution for the Tzomet withdrawal, and joint-list
faction counts the UI shows as "Unknown" although the official record resolves them.
