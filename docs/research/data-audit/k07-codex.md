Audited 2026-11-06 against worktree HEAD `68b836a` (fast-forward check against `origin/main`: already up to date). Election: 28 October 1969, seventh Knesset. Auditor only: no data edits, dataset build, push or integration merge.

## Summary and counting convention

**OK 30; WRONG 5; UNSUPPORTED 2; UNVERIFIABLE 1; DISPUTED 1.** Counts are grouped audit units: L01–L16 are one unit per electoral list, O01–O14 are supplementary checks, and F01–F09 are non-OK units. They are not counts of individual JSON scalars. A list row covers the fields explicitly named below; remaining evidence limits are not silently counted as confirmation.

The national results, ballot letters, gazetted list names, 120 elected names and candidate order agree with the retained primary notices. Fresh Knesset membership data agrees with the retained 134 membership-position records, including all 120 opening-day members. The 102-seat initial coalition and the 90–10–4 confidence vote are sound. The main repairs concern a Gahal minister undercount, omitted coalition change, stale evidence metadata, and unsupported consultation bounds. There is no demonstrated incorrect vote or seat total.

This is strongest on priorities 1–3 of the brief. Full live inspection of every biography and party-link destination and exhaustive discovery of later Gazette corrections were not completed; F08 and the coverage notes preserve those limits. No other auditor's report was read.
