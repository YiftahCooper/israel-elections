## Summary

Ledger counts: **OK 34 · WRONG 8 · UNSUPPORTED 2 · UNVERIFIABLE 8 · DISPUTED 0**. These are grouped findings/checks; see the explicit incomplete scope below.

## Remaining scope and challenge checks

* **Gazette retention/corrections:** G and C are retained and G matches fresh official bytes. A is a retained disclosed mirror. A includes an important cancellation and candidate resignations previously overlooked. Searches for 1996 / 4414 / תיקון טעות did not locate a formal later results correction; this is not proof none exists. The project's conflicting-source context event is appropriately unresolved. Do not silently replace 767401 with 767951, or 226275 with IDI's 226775. Full search of every later Gazette issue was not performed.
* **Published roster coverage:** all displayed candidate names/order were visually compared with the registered prefix and elected notice. Punctuation and spelling normalization is not a substitute for identity; O independently confirms all elected PersonIDs. Full zero-seat rosters and later ranks were not transcribed, by design. F05 contradicts an affirmative completeness claim, rather than merely requesting expanded coverage.
* **Aftermath gaps:** `reaction.gap` truthfully records absent balanced reactions; empty reactions not a false historical claim. `gaps[0]` remains research-process wording (“not retrieved”); G now establishes certification/publication and O independently gives opening membership. Update this gap when adopting the new evidence. Pending helicopter/tunnel/Bar-On entries were not promoted to confirmed events by this audit. Full evaluation of their two-chronology inclusion tests remains outstanding.
* **Editorial/source checks:** no English Wikipedia URL found in the election-specific records inspected; automatically surfaced search hits and mirrors were excluded. Hebrew Wikipedia-only constituent evidence is lower priority than reachable official/research sources; F12–16 preserve uncertainty instead of rubber-stamping it. No partisan wording defect found in the checked election/PM/context/aftermath text. Colour convention claims are not official party-colour claims; the two concrete wrong-party citations are F09–10.
* **Incomplete lower-priority scope:** F17–18 are grouped outstanding claims, not claims that every link or family assignment is false. Remaining colour entries' live wiki-convention values, complete party-link and biography targets, family definitions and general-party historical claims need a further pass. Thus counts must not be presented as a complete atomic census of everything the app holds.

No owner decision is needed to settle F01–08: primary records or direct local/HTTP evidence decide them. F09–18 require better evidence or additional auditing, not user ratification of historical facts.
