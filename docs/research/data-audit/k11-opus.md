# Data audit k11 (23 Jul 1984, 11th Knesset): Opus auditor

Auditor: Claude Opus 5.5. Checked on 6 November 2026 against branch `t3/d99ea6fc` at 2e15191 (contains 53cca36). This report only checks and reports; it changes no data.

Method:
- I read both retained official gazettes myself: the results notice, YP 3081 pp. 3006–3008, and the candidate-list notice, YP 3073 pp. 2826–2839. I re-downloaded both from their live URLs and the bytes are identical to the retained files (sha256 `1ce31396…` and `3b2626bf…`). The scans are CCITT fax images. I decoded them at full resolution and zoomed into every list letter and list name that differed from the data.
- I recomputed the allocation independently: 1% threshold, quota, Bader–Ofer, the four agreements.
- I compared the 120 elected names and all 139 bio Knesset IDs with Knesset OData (`KNS_PersonToPosition`, `KNS_MkSiteCode`).
- I checked the recommendations, the formation and the rotation against the JTA Daily Bulletin of 6 Aug, 14 Sep and 17 Sep 1984, and the PM tenure dates against Knesset OData (PositionID 45).
- Hebrew Wikipedia was used only as a lead and as a labelled cross-check (rank 5).

The votes, seats, totals and the 120 elected members are correct and match the official notice exactly. The errors are in the identity of the small lists:

- **Five ballot letters are misread** from the scan: הם→**הס**, טן→**טס**, פט→**כט**, נ→**ס**, עי→**עד** (Morasha). Both official notices print the correct letters, and so does the alphabetical order in which the notices list them. Hebrew Wikipedia agrees. The wrong letters are also baked into the list ids (`11-הם`, `11-טן`, `11-פט`, `11-נ`, `11-עי`), which appear in several files.
- **Four list names are misread**: "לבא אליאב" → **"לובה אליאב לכנסת"**; "עולי ההר" → **"עולי הודו"**; "עזרא זהר" → **"עזרה זהר"**; "א.נ.י." → **"א. נ."**.
- **List ע is labelled "Arab Democratic Party" (מד״ע).** It was the "ארגון ארצי להגנת הדייר", a national tenants' protection organisation. The Arab Democratic Party was founded in 1988. The label comes from `scripts/lib/list-meta.ts`. It is repeated in `parties.json`, and the party-links file points to the Arab Democratic Party's Hebrew Wikipedia article. A note in `parties.json` (`misleading-11-ay`) already documents the problem, but it was never fixed.
- **List ש is labelled "Sheli" (שלי)**, and the families and lineage files treat it as the Sheli party, a Labour/Zionist-left party. Sheli did not run in 1984. List ש was "שילוב – רשימה עצמאית של עולי הודו לכנסת", a list of immigrants from India.
- **Two stale "gap" notes in the aftermath file.** The certification date is in the retained notice: 31 July 1984, gazetted 1 Aug 1984. The first sitting was on 13 Aug 1984 (OData). The 21-day mandate extension was granted on 26 Aug 1984 (contemporaneous press).

### Other remarks (not counted)

- **The results notice has a misprint, and the data handles it correctly.** For list זה the results notice prints «בראשית ח"כ מרדכי בן־פורת». The candidate notice (YP 3073 p. 2830) prints «בראשות», which is what the data uses. Consider adding a variant note like the Speiser one.
- **Shas #4.** Both notices print «שלמה שמעון» (YP 3073 p. 2838; YP 3081 p. 3008). The Knesset (OData PersonID 2209) has «בן-שלמה שמעון», and the app shows English "Shimon Ben-Shlomo". This is consistent with the "gazette spelling kept" rule. A candidate note in the style of the Speiser note would explain the mismatch to readers.
- **Temporary-provisions law.** The agreements notice cites «סעיף 34א לחוק הבחירות לכנסת האחת־עשרה (הוראות שעה), התשמ"ד–1984» (ס"ח התשמ"ד עמ' 133, 166). The rule-set note names only the 1969 law and the 1973 amendment. The allocation still reproduces exactly, so this is documentation only.
- **Provenance labels.** In `aftermath/k11.json`, `sources["knesset-history"]` and `sources["government22"]` are Jewish Virtual Library mirrors labelled `kind: "official"`. The underlying facts are confirmed by OData: PM rows (PositionID 45) for Peres, PersonID 456, run 1984-09-13 → 1986-10-20, and for Shamir, PersonID 2652, from 1986-10-20. But the host is secondary, and the label could say so.
- **Non-winning rosters.** All 11 non-winning lists are marked `candidatesUnavailable`, but their full rosters are in the retained YP 3073. This is a scope gap, not an error.
- **Editorial stance.** Nothing partisan found. Kach and the PLP are described neutrally and with attribution. No English Wikipedia URL appears in any k11 file (grep of `k11.json`, aftermath, bios, logos, party-links, `public/data/k11.json`, `data/raw/k11/*`). No reversed Hebrew parentheses found.
