# k03 independent Codex audit

Audited 2026-11-06, on `t3/review-data-audit-k3`, baseline `5f2a4f3` (contains `53cca36`). `git merge --ff-only origin/main` completed: already up to date. Auditor role only: no data edits, dataset builds or browser test suites.

**Counts: OK 32; WRONG 10; UNSUPPORTED 1; UNVERIFIABLE 5; DISPUTED 0.** Counts refer to the numbered verification bundles below, not individual scalar fields. In particular the 18 list bundles cover all 18 vote/seat rows and 120 declared winners, while unresolved bundles explicitly cover many individual links or translations. This is not a claim that every biography destination, English spelling or colour attribution has been independently established.

The national vote totals and all 120 seats are sound. The material defects are the missing withdrawn list יז, wrong letters סא instead of סצ, wrong words in list names, and an anachronistic National Religious Party display label. A stale dataset hash and copied aftermath caveats also need repair.

## Evidence and method

All sources below accessed **2026-11-06**. Official retained PDFs were opened and rendered; the numbers, names, headers and notice dates were read from images rather than trusting OCR. Candidate pages 1–3, 5, 7, 9–10, 12–14, 16, 18 and 22 cover the transcribed winning prefixes. All three results pages and the signed five-pair notice were inspected. Candidate and results spellings differ in places; an identity-preserving candidate-gazette spelling is not automatically an error.

Independent discovery additionally reached YP 429 (withdrawals and a formal candidate-name correction), read its pages 1087–1088, and checked the contents pages of YP 428, 430 and 431. This is a bounded adjacent-issue check, not proof that no later correction or appeal ever existed. The retained government-formation citations were independently downloaded and read, including full confidence-vote pages. Live Knesset membership pagination returned **100 + 36 records**, no remaining next link; every retained membership field that exists in the live records matched. The opening snapshot contains 120 records. Seven constituent-faction queries were also read in full (no continuation links).

Machine downloads used the prescribed project User-Agent. Reproducible downloads, Python PDF tooling (`pymupdf 2.1`) and intermediate renders are disposable in `.backup-scratch/k03-audit/`. No raw source is promoted by this report. Newly useful evidence is identified by exact URL, printed page and quote below so the fixer can retain it through the normal manifest workflow. No challenges or access restrictions were circumvented. English Wikipedia was neither opened nor used; unsolicited search hits were ignored.

## Conflicts, boundaries and follow-up evidence

- **Electorate disagreement, resolved for this dataset:** the indexed Knesset results summary says **1,057,609**, while the rendered CBS table and independently opened IDI table say **1,057,795**, the app value. Retain CBS’s specialist statistical figure; record the conflicting Knesset summary rather than replacing the app value with an apparent typo. No count of “DISPUTED” is assigned because the app value has the better-supported provenance. The gazette does not give registered/cast totals, only list votes and invalid ballots.
- **Formal notices matter:** YP 429 item 6 records the complete יז withdrawal; items 1–5 are individual withdrawals for יט #7, ח #42, צח #6/#3 and ש #92. None displaces any of the 120 elected prefixes currently stored. P.1088 corrects the צח head’s name. A future losing-roster transcription must apply those notices rather than copying YP 425 uncritically. Do not mark an entire still-participating list withdrawn because one candidate withdrew.
- **Consultations:** the JTA reports support Herut’s refusal to attend and the National Religious Front asking for the largest party to be tasked without naming a personal nominee. Party conditions and expected coalition participation are not personal recommendations. The 120 “unknown” seats are not a claim that nobody recommended Ben-Gurion. No unsupported personal tally should be synthesized.
- **Term versus election window:** official history and member terms give 15 August 1955–30 November 1959. The aftermath explicitly defines its window as polling day to next polling day and admits no dissolution-law finding. There is no evidence here for changing that window to a guessed dissolution date. Preserve both meanings clearly. Prime-minister dates in `$.government.primeMinisters` are scoped to government 7, not to Ben-Gurion’s entire career; they do not assert he ceased being PM when government 8 began.
- **Pending Wadi Salib rationale can be revisited:** a rank-1 publisher’s historical journal now supplies a direct dated discussion: [Police History 2, Yoram Shachar](https://www.policemuseum.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Police-History-2-2020-Yoram-Shachar.pdf), p.157, “ב-9 ביולי 1959 התחוללו בשכונת ואדי סאליב בחיפה מהומות”. This was found in indexed text, not fully read. It challenges the mirrored “9 May” date and is a concrete next source for the pending event; it is not a reason to silently promote an unread article or change the inclusion rule. The pending grenade event is likewise distinguished from included events.
- **No sweeping certification of sources:** no English Wikipedia references were found in the inspected k3-specific records. Hebrew Wikipedia remains sole or partial evidence for some personal affiliations, colour conventions and link labels; the official faction queries corroborate most constituent identities but do not turn a shared-faction record into a party register. Partisan platforms are labelled own-party material. Cross-election family ideology and historical continuity assertions were not independently researched as an all-era audit; only k3 appearances, constituents, seat arithmetic and source hashes were checked here. Their external historical proof remains outside the OK bundles above.
