# k02 independent data audit — Codex

Audited 2026-11-06 against HEAD `5f2a4f3` (contains `53cca36`); `git merge --ff-only origin/main` returned “Already up to date.” Election: 30 July 1951, Second Knesset. Auditor only: no data edits or dataset build. This report covers the released national dataset and the related research overlays, including the aftermath draft which explicitly says it is not wired into the app.

**Counts: OK 54; WRONG 4; UNSUPPORTED 1; UNVERIFIABLE 4; DISPUTED 2.** These count the labelled **check groups** below: 17 numerical result rows (R), 15 Hebrew roster-prefix rows (C), 22 other checks (O), and 11 non-OK findings (N). They are not counts of individual JSON scalars. A numerical result row may be OK while its identity field has a separate finding. This is a broad inventory with explicit incomplete verification, especially English spellings and destination biography pages; it is not a claim that every displayed scalar has been independently proved.

The vote totals and all 120 published seats check out. The most consequential proven data error is the use of **ה instead of ו** for Hapoel HaMizrahi, which propagates into IDs, biographies, blocs and family records. Two primary-source disagreements need to be preserved rather than silently normalized. No corrected-results notice was identified in the bounded search; this does not establish that none exists.

## Evidence and method

All access dates in this report are **2026-11-06**. PDF page numbers are 1-based. Hebrew scans were rendered locally with PyMuPDF and read visually; OCR was used only to find text. Network retrievals used the project's prescribed User-Agent. Ordinary web-tool denials and successful workspace HTTP reads are distinguished; no challenge, login, paywall or regional routing was bypassed. Working copies and renders are in project-local `.backup-scratch/data-audit/`; URLs, inspected pages and relevant hashes are recorded here so scratch need not be retained.

The search was divided into results/legal rules, every retained roster prefix, formation/recommendations, and metadata/provenance. Challenge checks included the entire 17-list notice, candidate/result identity differences, the CEC memorandum, live OData pagination, known cross-election error classes and local content hashes. English Wikipedia search hits were ignored, never opened or used. No other auditor's election report was consulted.
