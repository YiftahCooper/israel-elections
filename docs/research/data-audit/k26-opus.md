# Data audit k26 (27 Oct 2026, 26th Knesset): Opus auditor

Auditor: Claude Opus 5.5. Checked on 6 November 2026 against `origin/main` at 53cca36. This report only checks and reports; it changes no data.

Method: I recomputed every count from the live CEC CSVs, re-ran the seat allocation independently in Python, and compared the 120 elected names with the Knesset OData service. I read the surplus-agreement gazette PDF myself. Recommendations and formation were checked against Kan, N12, Walla, Maariv, Ynet, Times of Israel, JNS and Channel 14, so that both sides of the political spectrum are represented. gov.il pages and president.gov.il were reached only through the Exa fetch service: gov.il returns HTTP 403 to scripts and president.gov.il shows a Sucuri JavaScript challenge, which I did not try to get around.

The results data is correct. Every vote count, total and seat matches the CEC's official final results page, and all 120 elected names match the Knesset. The errors are elsewhere:

- **Ra'am's disqualification is attached to the wrong list.** Three context events say that "HaShutafut LaKulam" (list ד) was disqualified and reinstated, and that its candidate #34 was struck off. All three facts belong to Ra'am (list עם). As displayed, the app attributes a disqualification to a list that was never disqualified.
- **The recommendations round is stale.** Likud recommended Netanyahu on 5 Nov at the last consultation. Herzog's own final tally was 67 for Eisenkot, 46 for Netanyahu and 7 abstaining (UTJ). He said he would task a candidate on Sunday 8 Nov. The data still shows Likud as "pending" and Netanyahu's total as 19.
- **The outgoing-coalition scheme is unsupported.** It counts UTJ as a member of the outgoing coalition and says UTJ "left and returned". The press record shows UTJ left the coalition in July 2025. I could not find a return before the election.
- **The publication status is out of date.** The CEC now publishes "תוצאות סופיות רשמיות" (official final results) with seats, and its plenary approved them on 4 Nov. The app still leaves `officialSeats` null and says the seats come from "press reports".

### Other remarks (not counted)

- **Completeness gaps.** `election-context.json` has a withdrawal event for Ahi (צבי) but none for Blue and White (כן), which withdrew on 21 Oct 2026. The CEC statement is quoted by Maariv (https://www.maariv.co.il/breaking-news/article-1379735): "היום (ד׳, 21 באוקטובר 2026), לאחר שכל המועמדים ברשימת 'כחול לבן בראשות בני גנץ' התפטרו כדין, הרשימה לא תתמודד". There is also no eligibility event for Avraham Shmuel Leibler (ט #31), although the list notes carry it.
- **Cosmetic English names.** Some names copied from the Knesset site have double spaces or odd forms: "Miri Miriam  Regev", "Yitshak  Goldknop", "Michael  Malkieli", "Youssef  Atauna", "Yitzhak  Kroizer", "Issac Shimon Wasserlauf", "Makhluf Miki Zohar".
- **`id` slug.** The `list-excluded-k26-ודעם` events are correctly tied to `26-ודם`, but the id has a stray "ע".
- **Interim vs final figures.** The CEC press release of 30.10.2026 (https://www.gov.il/he/pages/results-26) gives interim figures (5,716,665 counted, threshold 184,947). The app correctly uses the later final figures (5,717,397 cast; threshold 184,972 computed from 5,691,426 valid). Haaretz's 29.10 figures (5,704,141 cast) are also interim.
- **Logos (`data/meta/logos/k26.json`).** I read the evidence fields; I did not re-research the images. Every claim states its basis and caveats: Commons file dates, official-site asset paths, or live official headers downloaded on 4 Nov and labelled as post-poll. "די" is honestly `unresolved`. I found no overclaim.
- **Party links (`data/meta/party-links/k26.json`).** There are 28 neutral links to Hebrew Wikipedia and 3 to IDI, against 25 partisan party-site links. The party sites spot-checked (Otzma, RZ, Orot HaShahar, Noam) return HTTP 200. Hebrew Wikipedia is used as labelled secondary because Knesset 26 faction pages do not yet exist (the Knesset opens on 10 Nov). Once they appear, the fixer could replace Hebrew Wikipedia with Knesset faction pages.
- **Editorial stance.** Nothing partisan found in the k26 text. The aftermath notes attribute claims neutrally. No English Wikipedia URL appears in any k26 file (grep of `k26.json`, the aftermath, bios, party-links and logos files, and `public/data/k26.json`).
