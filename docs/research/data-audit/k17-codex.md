## Roster, subsidiary metadata and coverage limits

- **719 candidates held in the app; 120 elected names/ranks independently checked.** Every elected rank was read in the results gazette; every elected bio PersonID belongs to the live opening membership set. Later candidates are not interchangeable with initial winners. C’s pages 2184–2201 were visually inspected for list headings, terminal ranks, withheld glyphs and wrapping, but this report does **not** certify a fresh 599-name character-by-character transcription. Remaining unelected spelling/order/English transliteration checks are UNVERIFIABLE within F10/F11 coverage limits, not additional counted findings.
- **Wrapping tests:** `17-כן#21` is `ברזניץ שלמה (יוראי)` with correctly oriented parentheses (C p.2192 / G p.2467). `17-ל#7` has `אהרון-` wrapped under `לנדבר` in the surname column (C p.2193); it is **not** stray text from rank 8. G p.2467 assigns `לנדבר אהרון` to rank 7 and `אהרונוביץ` to rank 8. The existing explicit name-variant note preserves that distinction correctly. `17-ו#3` and `17-טב#9` also retain documented differences between the two official notices; do not “correct” them by normalizing only one source. No reversed-parenthesis defect was found among held candidate names.
- **Unavailable rosters:** כ, חץ, יש, נץ, קפ, ף are openly missing, with exact unresolved ranks. C and, for some lists, the IDI-hosted CEC copies offer recovery routes. A readable new transcription would be useful, but an acknowledged coverage gap is not a wrong elected roster. Seven winning lists are explicitly truncated; the status note reports those counts accurately.
- **Affiliations:** supplied elected split is Labor/Meimad 18/1; Moledet/Tkuma/Ahi/NRP 2/2/2/3; Aguda/Degel 4/2; UAL/Ta’al/Mada 2/1/1. Arithmetic/rank linkage is correct. The later Degel quote “3 of 6” does not change the opening split 4/2. F11 distinguishes plausible sourced affiliations from independently verified pre-election constituent identities.
- **Links:** gazette-based links point to the correct whole-election record and say so. NRP links to מפד"ל, rather than a later Jewish Home/Religious Zionist party. Tkuma/Ahi have empty constituent links, which is a disclosed absence, not a later-party substitution. Platform links are intentionally partisan and labelled as such; no opinion content was used as historical proof. F15 covers unvisited destinations.
- **Families/lineage/party definitions:** inspected all nine k17 family appearances and four partner edges, including their source/interpretation qualifiers. Vote/member IDs and mixed whole-list calculations agree with the released results. Their ideological classifications and cross-election narratives remain editorial inferences; this audit does not independently certify every underlying party-history claim in shared global definitions. No invented constituent vote totals or official ideological bloc was found.
- **Colours:** app palette choices are not historical facts. Medium-confidence colour entries explicitly describe Hebrew Wikipedia conventions. F06/F07 concern defective supporting attribution, not a mandate to replace display colours. All other colour hex/source-template assertions were inspected locally but not independently fetched one by one.
- **Logos:** checked declared provenance only, as instructed, without re-researching images. Commons supports both qualified descriptions, at community evidence strength. - **UI / source policy:** searched `src/` for k17 and 2006; found threshold chronology and null-locality-code commentary, no additional election-specific narrative or misattached list event.
