## Source conflicts, editorial checks and limits

- **Resolved by rank, not DISPUTED:** IDI Hebrew/English tables give Workers Bloc3205 and Traditional Judaism241; [R] prints3214 and239. Keep the app’s gazette values and document the secondary discrepancy. All other per-list vote totals match IDI. IDI English rounds turnout87.0% and says13 ministers; Hebrew gives86.9% and12 ministers. The official CBS ratio is86.878% (86.9% to one decimal); the initial official cabinet has12 ministers. These external errors must not replace primary values. No app change is needed for those values.
- **Term versus interval:** the aftermath explicitly defines its closing date as next election, so I did not report30July1951 as an erroneous parliamentary end date. Actual service interval is14February1949–20August1951, independently visible in OData. The dissolving/election-date act and its passage day were not established here; do not substitute a recess or next-election day for that act.
- **Government chronology:** Government1’s resignation and successor’s approval are different events. The existing end1950-11-01 does not skip another prime minister: Rosen’s role is expressly a formation assignment, not a premiership. A complete dated government2 end/ministerial-change history is not present in the draft; no invented day added.
- **Logo evidence:** eight Commons descriptions, source attributions and displayed licensing sections were read; only the existing evidence was checked. These are community metadata, outside the hierarchy’s enumerated official/research/news ranks; they are not independent primary campaign records. WIZO/Hatzohar pages display CC BY-SA3.0; Fighters/Haredi/Popular Arab Bloc/Working and Religious Women/For Jerusalem show PD-textlogo; Gruenbaum shows PD-Israel and PD-textlogo. Fighters’ structured data disagrees with its displayed licensing section; retain that metadata ambiguity rather than claim a legal determination. Six exact repaired URLs are in F05–F10. - **Hebrew-Wikipedia dependence:** first-coalition and Nazareth-affiliation fields currently rely partly on rank5 even though opened rank2 IDI evidence supports them (O43).
