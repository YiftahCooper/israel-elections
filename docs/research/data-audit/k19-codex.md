## Evidence provenance and limits for reconciliation

- **Correction reproducibility:** downloaded `https://olaw.org.il/yalkut/yalkut-6546.pdf` with the project User-Agent; HTTP200, SHA-256 `997628f9f5b6d556b62e2547489cbc9b6fbd64fa721c6eff687577ae1074cc24`. Last PDF page, printed p.2874, bottom-left correction. The text layer inserts spurious `2` glyphs between words; the quoted number replacement is unambiguous. The CEC official history index independently identifies this exact issue/page/date. Temporary PDF/extraction copies are in project `.backup-scratch/k19-audit/`; fixer should durably retain the accepted correction in raw inputs and manifest.
- **Results source hierarchy:** gazette beats informal summaries, but the repository explicitly represents the final CEC figures and discloses conflicting statutory numbers. No newly read source attributes the three list deltas to a court order or particular polling stations. Do not convert a known total typo correction into an invented list-level explanation.
- **Roster identities:** 17 candidate-name strings differ from the elected-results notice (including shortened names, reordered forenames and spelling variants).
