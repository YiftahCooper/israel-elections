# k06 independent Codex data audit

## Summary and counting rule

This is a **partial audit with explicit unresolved coverage**, not a certification that every metadata fact is correct. Counts are independently assessed **claim groups/table rows**, not individual scalar fields or 120 separate names. Result rows check votes/seats and separately identify name/letter exceptions; roster rows cover every elected name per list. Unsupported/unverifiable groups do not become OK by internal consistency.

**OK: 44**, **WRONG: 11**, **UNSUPPORTED: 2**, **UNVERIFIABLE: 5**, **DISPUTED: 1**.

The most consequential new findings are three wrong ballot-letter transcriptions (Peace, Brotherhood movement, Young Israel), a materially wrong Brotherhood list name, and Eshkol being shown as PM after his death with Allon omitted. Votes and all 120 published seats match the inspected official notice. An official electorate disagreement is preserved rather than silently settled.

## Coverage and evidence limitations

- Read the retained YP 1230 results notice visually (PDF pp.1–3, printed pp.311–313); remaining PDF pages are scan/back-cover certification material, not an extra correction notice. Results notice is retained and the official source manifest hashes match its identity. Checked YP 1222 winning prefixes and losing-list headings, and YP 1225 signed connection notice. **No independent exhaustive search of later correction notices** was completed. The date on the retained candidate cover is 17 October 1965 and its first printed page is 113 (do not inherit an indexed CEC summary's 133 page typo).
- Candidate names/election status: all 120 elected identities/order compared to YP 1230; live membership read in two pages, not just the first 100. Existing candidate-notes file has no k6 entry. No pre-opening replacement mismatch appeared. This does not establish every later replacement or full losing roster. Winning rosters truthfully expose truncation; the candidate notice contains later ranks and nonwinning candidates the app does not claim to reproduce.
- Gazette name fidelity: modern short labels may use modern orthography, but `nameHe` represents the official name. F06–F07 concern that field. Other checked name differences were punctuation/spacing, not a new word. English short labels were inspected as editorial translations, not certified official English names; transliterating a misread name is still wrong (F02).
- Government evidence: retrieved government13 presentation, full 12 January 1966 record, full 17 March 1969 record and 6 March 1969 memorial sitting with project User-Agent. Read the membership API with the same UA. Hebrew PDF extraction is not reliable reading order; its quoted excerpts were checked for meaning. Meir's cabinet number is corroborated by its relation to government13 and the recorded official government chronology, but a complete government14 coalition snapshot remains outside this pass.
- Recommendations: JTA source actually fetched successfully with project UA and dated 26 November. It confirms **two** factions only. No primary presidential per-faction diary was reached, no additional recommendation inferred from coalition or confidence vote, no claim that `unknown` means abstention. The app's `data/meta/k6.json` currently has **no pm-recommendations scheme**; recommendation claims audited above are the aftermath research input.
- Party constituent memberships: individually inherited historical-faction citations and empty evidence arrays are not contemporary proof. F16 preserves this limitation for all member assignments, including the Ruth Haktin conflict note. No independent ideological/family-membership review beyond the constituent identities and dataset hashes was completed. Family inclusion is an editorial classification and must not be conflated with a formal 1965 electoral bloc. Affiliated Arab-list scheme was inspected; independent confirmation of each affiliation claim remains outstanding.
- Metadata/bios/link review is incomplete (F16–F18). All 120 bio PersonIDs were compared, but destination URLs were not all opened. No English Wikipedia citation/link was found in k6-specific inputs; search-engine results that happened to include English Wikipedia were not followed or used. Hebrew Wikipedia is the sole basis for colours and some affiliation assignments; official/academic constituent and coalition sources reachable above should replace Wikipedia-only support where they cover the fact. Party platforms are legitimate evidence of their own claims, not independent validation of policies.
- Logo check is limited to the existing evidence, as requested; no image re-research. Alignment metadata describes the actual Commons page and identifies a source, but campaign use is still inherited. Rafi's own evidence openly admits no contemporary artefact. Rakah Commons fetch failed. The Alignment and Rakah stored sourceUrl values omit `File:`; corrected description-page URLs are supplied above. No evidence establishes that any of these is a later redesign, so none is labelled as such.
- The historical allocation algorithm behind `il-1951-largest-remainder-1` was not independently rederived from the law in this pass. IDI confirms 1% and official seats are all checked. Public `ballotBoxes:0`, `localities:0` are missing-coverage sentinels with `hasLocalities:false`, not independently verified historical counts of zero; UI interpretation should preserve that distinction.
- The term object is used as an election-cycle window. Its `end=1969-10-28` must not be presented as the institutional end of Knesset 6: live membership rows end 17 November 1969. No dissolving-act date is asserted by this report; normal expiry needs the original legal source (F19). Reaction statements/issue polls are explicitly uncollected, not evidence that none existed. War-date gap text is demonstrably stale (F11); other coverage gaps must remain.
- Scope searches included k6 and 1965 in raw manifests, public data, all metadata families and src. No dedicated k6/1965 factual UI sentence was found. No domestic partisan wording requiring correction was established by the inspected records.
