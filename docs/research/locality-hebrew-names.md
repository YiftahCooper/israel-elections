# Hebrew locality display names

Generated by `scripts/build-locality-hebrew-names.ts`; regeneration instructions are in that script.
Inputs: retained official CEC election rows in `public/data/k*-localities.json` and official CBS lists in `data/raw/demographics/cbs-*` (2003–2024), parsed by `scripts/locality-map-cbs.py`. Source URLs and provenance remain in the repository's retained source manifests. No external or inferred names.

Policy: code-only join, nearest CBS year, earlier on ties; remove CBS footnote asterisks. Repair mirrored CEC parentheses before comparing, including an unmatched closing bracket from a cut-off qualifier. Historical transitions recorded in the resolver block substitution before prefix recovery (a merger may extend a complete old name). Accept punctuation equality, spelling equality (within-word doubled yod/vav, the explicit חרות/חירות variant, and an optional complete trailing שבט qualifier), or a nonempty strict punctuation-insensitive CEC prefix of CBS. Everything else retains tidied CEC. No code means no CBS substitution. Unclosed qualifiers on retained conflicts are marked truncated and CBS is disclosed separately in the table and tooltip; no missing letters are invented. Original CEC rows remain untouched and searchable. Counts exclude envelopes. Parentheses repaired overlaps the other categories; truncation/spelling/rename/other are exclusive reasons. Kept (other) includes unmatched same-code names and rows without CBS code/name evidence; the latter are also broken out as a subset. They have no proposed CBS name to list in the conflict table. Changed compares display with exact CEC text.
