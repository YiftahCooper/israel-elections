## Reproduction and limits

Run `npm ci`, `npx tsc -p tsconfig.json --noEmit`, `node scripts/build-datasets.ts`,
`node scripts/check-colours.ts`, and `npx vitest run`. Build without `.backup-scratch/raw`
to simulate CI; retained inputs alone must suffice. pdfjs-dist 6.5.438 rendered scans with
its matching wasm directory; routine renders and downloads remain in ignored `.backup-scratch/`.
No backup-system exclusion is asserted. Existing non-K8–K12 JSON timestamp-only changes
are restored before commit.

Legal authorities recovered: original SH556 §§67,81–84 and SH692 Amendment4; no new
1977 cap statute/CEC decision found. Bounded searches and missing evidence are recorded
in 0073–0074. The complete intervening amendment chain, all later candidate withdrawals,
and capacity-sensitive counterfactuals involving unknown full rosters remain unverified.

Rules UI: no app edit. Proposed follow-up label/limitations are described in 0073.
