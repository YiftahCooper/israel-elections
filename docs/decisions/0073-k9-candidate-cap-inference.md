# K9 candidate capacity: successful hypothesis, explicit legal uncertainty

The owner authorized testing and, on exact reproduction, using this hypothesis:
cap each list at its known full candidate count, then continue the section 81(d)
highest-quotient remainder procedure, retaining section 82 pair units. Cap whole-quota
allocations as well as remainder allocations; sum partner capacities nationally and
apply individual capacities during internal re-division. No vote is changed.

`RuleSet.seatsCappedAtCandidates` is optional and off when absent/false. Only the
K9 preset enables it. `ListInput.candidateCount` supplies a known complete count;
unknown counts are unbounded, never equated to the length of a partial transcription.
Scenario conversion therefore supplies counts only for complete available rosters.
The complete one-candidate פש roster is sufficient to establish the baseline K9 cap;
other winning lists have reviewed prefixes long enough to hold their published seats.
This does **not** certify capacity-sensitive counterfactuals with unknown full rosters.
Scenario export/import, historical validation and departure detection carry the flag.
No `src/app/` files were changed. A future Rules panel option should say “Cap seats at
known candidate counts”, explain the inference for 1977, and disclose unbounded unknown
rosters; it should be editable only in experimental mode like the other rules.

Using all six published agreements, the optional procedure reproduces **every list**:
פש **2 → 1**, ש **1 → 2**; all others unchanged, including NRP **12**. Both tables are
retained in 0075 and tested. The uncapped calculation remains available with the option
off; other historical presets retain it off. Independent reference tests cover initial
caps, exhausted capacity and pair-unit/internal division. Full test-suite evidence is
recorded in the implementation report.

The plenum then voted: "הצעת ההחלטה של ועדת הכנסת נתקבלה".

**In English (summary).** Gross argued that the Central Elections Committee (CEC) should not have approved
Flatto-Sharon as the only candidate of his list: the list won enough votes for at least two seats, so the
last member seated from the surplus distribution should be removed. The House Committee found that the CEC
had acted under section 81 of the 1969 Elections Law: in the first stage it gave the Flatto-Sharon list
the most seats it could take, one; 114 seats were distributed that way; the six seats left over were
distributed under section 81(d) (Bader–Ofer). There was no other lawful way, and the CEC must seat 120
members. The list's extra votes were not lost: they counted for the threshold (81(a)) and the quota
(81(b)). A one-candidate list is valid. The Knesset adopted the proposal and rejected the appeal.

**What it settles.**
- The procedure this decision implements is the one the CEC actually used in 1977, not only a hypothesis
  that happens to reproduce the result: cap the list at its one candidate in the first stage, then give
  the remaining seats by the section 81(d) remainder procedure. The Knesset accepted it as lawful when it
  rejected the appeal.
- The capped list's votes stay in the threshold and quota calculation, as in the implementation (no vote
  is changed).
- The sentence above, "the procedure is inferred from the officially published result because the
  statutory text was not located", is therefore outdated for 1977: the procedure is documented. The
  Hebrew paragraph above is outdated in the same way.

**What it does not settle.**
- No statutory clause that sets the cap was located. The committee rests the result on section 81 and the
  duty to seat 120 members; section 83 only seats candidates in list order.
- The stage counts do not match the app exactly. The transcript says 114 seats in the first stage and six
  remainder seats. With the cap, the app's first stage counts whole quotas list by list and gives 113, then
  7 remainder seats (quota 14,173). Counting each surplus-agreement pair as one unit with its combined votes
  gives 114, as does a first stage with no cap. Final seats are identical for every list, so the published
  result and decision 0075 are unaffected. Which first-stage reading the 1973 law intends is open; the
  engine was not changed here. (Settled by the note below, 9 November 2026: the law gives 113 + 7.)
- How the cap applies inside a surplus-agreement pair (internal re-division) and in counterfactuals with
  unknown full roster sizes: the 1977 case had only a single-candidate list, so this source says nothing
  about them.

A separate change, not from this source: the record above says K9 stays unavailable because YP 2326 omits
the Ratz elected-member paragraph. Since the k9 audit (6 November 2026) K9 is available: Shulamit Aloni,
Ratz rank 1, is confirmed by official Knesset OData membership and faction records from the first sitting
(docs/research/data-audit/k09-resolution.md; status note in public/data/elections.json).

## Note, 9 Nov 2026: why the transcript says 114 and the app 113

**Result.** The law gives the app's split, 113 whole-quota seats and 7 remainder seats. The transcript's
114 + 6 copies one page of the Central Elections Committee's own calculation sheet, which counted each
surplus-agreement pair jointly in the first stage. The same sheet then hands out the 7 remaining seats
from 113, exactly as the app does. No code was changed. Every list's final seats are the same under
both counts.

**The law in 1977.** The 1969 consolidated law, SH 556 p.116 (retained as `data/raw/k8-k12/law-1969.pdf`,
PDF p.14). Amendment 4 (SH 692 p.108, `data/raw/research/law-bader-ofer-1973.pdf`) replaced only §81(d);
§81(c) and §82 kept their 1969 wording, which is word for word the wording in force today. Amendments 1–3
(1969–1973) were not re-read for this note.

> **81.** (ג) כל רשימה המשתתפת בחלוקת המנדטים תזכה במספר מנדטים כמספר השלם היוצא מחילוק קולותיה הכשרים במודד.
>
> (ד) [1973] המנדטים שנותרו אחרי חלוקת המנדטים לפי סעיף קטן (ג) יחולקו בין הרשימות המשתתפות בחלוקת המנדטים
> באופן זה: (1) לכל רשימה ייקבע "מודד רשימה" על ידי חילוק מספר הקולות הכשרים שקיבלה למספר המנדטים שזכתה בהם
> לפי סעיף קטן (ג) נוסף אחד; …
>
> **82.** (א) שתי רשימות מועמדים שהתקשרו ביניהן ייחשבו לענין חלוקת המנדטים לפי סעיף 81(ד) כרשימה אחת בעלת
> מספר הקולות הכשרים שקיבלו שתיהן יחד ובעלת המנדטים שזכו בהם שתיהן יחד, ובלבד שכל אחת מהן בפני עצמה קיבלה
> את אחוז הקולות הקבוע בסעיף 81(א).

In English: each list gets its own whole quotas (81(c)); a pair counts as one list only for the remainder
stage (81(d)), holding "the seats that both won together". So in 1977 the Alignment (430,023 votes) gets
30 and the Yemenite list (24,185) gets 1; the pair enters the remainder stage with 31, not with the 32 that
its combined 454,208 votes would give. With Flatto-Sharon capped at 1, the first stage totals 113.

- **1977.** Section ו (p.6, printed p.9) is a printed form with the pairs already named on it ("אמת + ימ");

  "ס"ה חולקו 114" and "נותרו לחלוקה 6". Section ח (pp.8–9, printed pp.12–13), handwritten and headed
  "חלוקת 7 המנדטים הנותרים", starts אמת+ימ at 31 and awards seven seats in this order, with these
  quotients: אמת+ימ 14,194; מחל+כנ 14,043; לע+יש 13,915; אמת+ימ 13,763; מחל+כנ 13,731; ב+ג 13,714;
  ש 13,640. Section ט (p.10, printed p.14) totals "7 מנדטים". The app's remainder walk is the same seven seats in the
  same order with the same quotients. The House Committee's 114 + 6 is section ו.
- Section ז of the 1977 sheet (p.7) writes the Independent Liberals (לע) as 21,277 votes and the pair
  as 223,542; sections ו and ח use 222,649, which is the gazette's 20,384 + 202,265. The app uses the
  gazette.

**Why both counts give the same seats.** Bader–Ofer gives each remaining seat to the highest
votes ÷ (seats + 1). A pair whose combined votes hold one more whole quota than its members' separate
quotas has a quotient above the quota (454,208 ÷ 32 = 14,194 > 14,173), so it wins that seat first in the
remainder stage. The seat moves between the two stages; the result does not.
