# K8–K12 release standard and remaining legal limitation

The owner's explicit release decision supersedes the **release gates**, not the
recorded observations, in 0070–0071. Every winning list needs official candidate
ranks 1..N, N at least its published seats, with a name-by-name comparison against
the elected members printed in the results notice. Later ranks are optional.
`candidatesTruncated: true` marks omitted later ranks; untranscribed nonwinning
lists have `candidatesUnavailable: true` and an empty candidate array. The flags
mean unavailable in this dataset, not absent from the archives.

Reviewed JSON retains printed page references, published roster sizes, contiguous
ranks, elected-name transcriptions and explicit spelling/alias variants. Results
that print given name before surname are normalized to surname/given order for
comparison. Names are never generated from modern membership lists.

The uncompleted point-in-time amendment chain is **a documented limitation, not a
release gate** when the verified original text reproduces all official seats.
Dataset status discloses roster scope and the legal limitation in Hebrew and English.
Subsequent withdrawals/corrections beyond the verified elected ranks are not claimed
reconstructed. These national-only datasets have `hasLocalities: false`; zero station
and locality counts are unavailable-data placeholders, not historical counts.

החלטת הבעלים מחליפה את תנאי השחרור ב־0070–0071: נדרש תמלול הדרגות הראשונות
בכל רשימה זוכה עד לפחות מספר המנדטים, והצלבת כל שם עם הודעת הנבחרים הרשמית.
דרגות מאוחרות הן רשות ומסומנות כחלקיות. שרשרת התיקונים שלא הושלמה היא מגבלה
מתועדת, ולא תנאי לשחרור כאשר כל המנדטים הרשמיים משוחזרים.
